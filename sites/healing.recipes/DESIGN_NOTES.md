# healing.recipes — design notes (for design-qa and future beauty passes)

Written 2026-07-16, first build. Brand law: Kairali tokens only (rev. 2 manual).

**Subject & job.** The official shelf of Gita Ramesh's four books. One job:
make a visitor pick a book up (buy-click, the KPI) and remember the author.

**The plan.**
- Palette: all token anchors — ivory ground, ink text, forest actions,
  forest-700 dark band, bronze rules/overlines. Marigold is spent exactly once
  on the light ground: the "New · 2026" tag on the hero Harmony cover (ink on
  marigold, 8.3:1). Marigold-300 on the dark band is the ch.08 dark-surface
  descriptor rule, not a second accent.
- Type: Kurale display (400 only) set like a title page; Asap body; Lato as
  the *bibliographic voice* — the site's structural device is the biblio line
  (`2000 · Roli Books · 84 pp`), real data doing the decorating.
- **Signature: THE SHELF.** A forest-700 dusk band where all four real covers
  stand bottom-aligned on a bronze shelf line. The landscape-format 2000 book
  stands shorter than the portrait books — like a real shelf. Chronology
  left→right is true information (2000 → 2019 → 2022 → 2026). Hover deepens
  the shadow only; nothing lifts or bounces (motion law).
- One orchestrated motion moment: the hero cover settles in (102%→100% fade,
  400ms) — the brand's logo-entrance gesture applied to a book being set down.
- Copy register: ED (editorial) — literate, specific, no filler, no health
  claims. Publisher blurb language with claims ("removing toxins", "knocking
  off fat") was deliberately NOT carried over; one short attributed
  endorsement quote appears on the Cookbook plate.

**Why it isn't the AI-default look.** Ivory + serif is brand law, not a
choice. The memory of the page lives in the dark shelf band (most Kairali
sites stay light throughout), the height-varied real covers, and the
bibliographic captions. No numbered markers, no gradient hero, no stat tiles.
