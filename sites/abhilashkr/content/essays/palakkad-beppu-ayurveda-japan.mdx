# Taking Kerala Ayurveda to Japan: Sourcing and Standards

Exporting traditional herbal products to strict markets like Japan demands absolute verification of botanical identity and safety metrics.

## The Quality Hurdle
Japanese regulations require extensive documentation of:
- Raw botanical identity.
- Pesticide and heavy metal clearance logs.
- WHO-GMP production trace maps.

## Building the Pipeline
We coordinate direct partnerships between Kerala cultivation sites and Japanese distribution networks, validating clinical standards at every supply chain link.
