# Running a 118-Year Ayurvedic House on AI Operations

Traditional medical lines face scaling limits. Admin overhead can dilute clinical focus, while manual record-keeping slows distribution tracking across international boundaries. 

## The Challenge of Legacy Data
Traditional medicine depends on diagnosis patterns. To scale this across Kairali's footprint of 30+ countries, we require high data standardisation without losing diagnostic integrity.

## Our AI Sourcing Protocol
We deploy local language models to parse:
- Botanical sourcing records.
- Batch processing logs for KAPPL manufacturing.
- Admin charting records at Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village (KTAHV) in Palakkad.

By digitizing and indexing these records, we verify compliance with WHO-GMP regulations and expedite administrative dispatch.
