    Welcome · An Award-Winning Wellness Retreat

    Refresh your mind, body and soul at Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village

    Welcome to Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village in Palakkad, Kerala. We are a certified Ayurvedic hospital and wellness retreat offering authentic Ayurvedic care, including physician-planned Panchakarma treatment, for inpatients and outpatients. Our 65-acre village is surrounded by lush greenery and coconut trees, providing a serene environment for recovery and rejuvenation.

    Read more

    With a legacy of 25 years, our skilled therapists provide personalized therapies, treatments, and medications based on the ancient wisdom of Ayurveda. Enjoy farm-to-table cuisine with organic, locally sourced ingredients, nourishing your body from within. Engage in daily yoga and meditation sessions for self-discovery and inner peace. Immerse yourself in nature and traditional healing practices at Kairali Ayurvedic Healing Village for a transformative wellness programme.

    Palakkad, Kerala — on India's south-west coast
