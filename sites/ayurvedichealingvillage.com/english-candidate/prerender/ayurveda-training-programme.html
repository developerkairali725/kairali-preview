The programme

Process Involved In Ayurveda Training Program

The Ayurveda Training Program at Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village is a comprehensive and immersive experience that introduces participants to the principles and practices of ancient Ayurveda. The process is meticulously designed to balance theoretical knowledge with practical application, ensuring holistic understanding and skill development.

01

Initial Orientation: The program begins with an orientation session to familiarize participants with the core concepts of Ayurveda, including its history, philosophy, and applications. Participants are introduced to the three doshas (Vata, Pitta, and Kapha) and their role in maintaining health.

02

Theory Sessions: In-depth classroom-style sessions cover topics such as: Ayurvedic anatomy and physiology, Basics of herbal medicine and Ayurvedic formulations, Principles of diet and lifestyle management.

03

Practical Training: Hands-on practice includes learning various Ayurvedic therapies like: Abhyanga (oil massage), Shirodhara (pouring warm oil on the forehead), Panchakarma (detoxification techniques), Participants also engage in preparing herbal remedies and Ayurvedic dishes to understand the practical applications of theoretical knowledge.

04

Yoga & Meditation Integration: To enhance learning, yoga and meditation are incorporated into the daily routine. These practices align with Ayurveda’s emphasis on mind-body harmony.

05

Personalized Feedback: Instructors provide regular feedback to ensure participants grasp the techniques correctly and refine their skills for effective implementation.

Features of the program

  • ◆Comprehensive Curriculum & Expert Faculty: Our program offers a broad Ayurvedic curriculum taught by seasoned practitioners, ensuring a well-rounded education.
  • ◆Practical Training & Customization: Gain hands-on experience through workshops and tailor your focus within Ayurveda, such as therapy, nutrition, or management.
  • ◆Interactive Learning & Holistic Approach: Engage in interactive sessions that explore Ayurveda’s holistic healing and global applications.

Learning outcomes

  • ◆Comprehensive Education: Deepen your Ayurvedic knowledge from basics to advanced levels.
  • ◆Practical Skills: Gain hands-on experience in Ayurvedic therapies.
  • ◆Tailored Learning & Holistic Approach: Customize your education and explore Ayurveda’s holistic approach.

Frequently asked questions

Who is this training program suitable for?

Our program is suitable for therapists, doctors, dieticians, managers, students, and Ayurveda enthusiasts who wish to deepen their knowledge and skills in Ayurveda.

What is the duration of the training program?

The listed course options run for 1, 3, 5, 10 or 20 days, with 3, 9, 15, 40 or 80 training hours respectively.

Can I specialize in a specific area of Ayurveda?

Our program allows you to customize your learning experience and focus on specific areas of interest within Ayurveda, such as therapy, nutrition, consultation, or management.

  • A 40% deposit secures the booking; the balance is due no later than 30 days before admission

Admission and discharge

  • Check-in from 12:00 noon; check-out by 10:00
  • Early check-in and late check-out are available on request, subject to availability, and may carry a charge

Cancellation

  • More than 30 days before admission: full refund
  • 15 to 30 days before admission: 50% of the amount paid is refunded, less applicable transaction fees and taxes
  • Fewer than 15 days before admission, or a change of dates during the stay: no refund

Children

  • Under 5 years: complimentary
  • 5 to 12 years: 50% of the meal charge; extra bed at the standard rate
  • Over 12 years: full charge
  • Parents or guardians must supervise children at all times. Parts of the facility are treatment areas and are not suitable for children
