Product Range

Our product line at Kairali Ayurvedic Group reflects more than a century of Ayurvedic knowledge and experience. Natural, carefully selected ingredients are used to create each composition to guarantee purity, effectiveness, and compatibility with the age-old Ayurvedic knowledge. Our products, which range from restorative oils to therapeutic powders, are made to address certain health issues and encourage overall well-being.

Products are not sold on this website. Each range can be bought from the Kairali product website, www.kairaliproducts.com, and from the hospital’s own pharmacy as prescribed by the doctors.
