Kairali Ayurvedic Village – Personalised Wellness Formula

Kairali Ayurvedic Group’s flagship ayurvedic destination, Kairali-The Ayurvedic Healing Village, Palakkad, Kerala is one-of-its-kind of a holistic space, which heals you internally and externally with traditional and authentic Ayurvedic therapies, treatments and medications along with Yoga and Meditation. A certified model of a wellness retreat, the healing village is implanted at the foot of Nilgiri and Annamalai ranges (Western Ghats). Set amidst mountains, forests, fertile valley and rivers are the 30 premium villas, which are forever surrounded by the fragrance of Pala trees. In compliance with Vaastu Shastra, the villas add on to the restorative and curative ambience. The Ayurvedic Healing Village also boasts of Red Oxide flooring, a rare Valambari Conch shell and a gentle stream flowing around every villa. Yoga, Meditation, Astrology, Palmistry, nutrition, therapy, spiritual training and traditional Indian rituals (pujas) are part of the routine at the retreat. The Ayurvedic Healing Village is the epitome of Kairali’s skill in concept hospitality – a perfectly intuitive design born of timeless wisdom. Time spent at this inspiring jungle sanctuary will leave you wondering whether you are experiencing the height of modern holistic luxury, or have travelled far into India’s distant past.

Our Founder’s Vision

We at Kairali wish to transform you to the optimal way of living.

Kairali’s mission is to spread the use of Ayurveda worldwide, upholding its authentic values to promote health and wellness for all. They see humans and nature as interdependent and believe Ayurveda can bring the body into a natural harmony. Kairali aims to be recognized as pioneers in the field, developing new natural elements to empower individuals and transform lives.

A letter from K.V. Ramesh

Dear Guests, 

Greetings from Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village, a haven where the ageless knowledge of Ayurveda is combined with the necessities of contemporary life. It gives me great pleasure to share with you the core values of Kairali, which are a dedication to holistic wellness, genuine Ayurvedic therapy, and the progress of humankind.

My vision for Kairali Ayurvedic Group has always been firmly based on the age-old science of Ayurveda, which I consider to be the best route to harmony and health. Growing up in a household immersed in Ayurvedic customs, I have personally seen the significant influence it has on people’s lives. This gave me the motivation to devote my life to protecting and introducing the world to this priceless legacy. 

Authenticity and excellence have always been the cornerstones of Kairali. This idea is reflected in Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village. From the therapies and treatments to the calm, environmentally friendly setting, every element of this retreat has been planned to offer a fully immersive Ayurvedic experience. Here, we establish a sanctuary where visitors can discover tranquility, renewal, and balance by fusing traditional customs with contemporary hospitality standards.

Our goal goes beyond providing Ayurvedic care. It is to encourage a way of living that values mental, spiritual, and physical well-being. We aim to enable our guests to live better, more satisfying lives through our customized therapies, therapeutic diets, yoga classes, and meditation techniques. 

Although Kairali Ayurvedic Group has become a well-known brand worldwide throughout the years, our principles have not changed. We are here to act as Ayurveda’s stewards, offering its therapeutic benefits to those who seek them while upholding the integrity that has established our reputation as a leading authority on holistic health.

To everyone who visits Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village, I extend my heartfelt gratitude. Your conviction in Ayurveda and Kairali motivates us to innovate and improve continuously. I welcome you to embark on this transforming journey of self-discovery, healing, and regeneration, guided by the timeless principles of Ayurveda. 

Together, let us create a healthier, happier world.

Warm regards,
K.V. Ramesh
Managing Director, Kairali Ayurvedic Group

Meet our Directors with Vision of Healthy World

  • K.V. RAMESH

    Managing Director

    As the harbinger of Ayurveda, Mr. K.V. Ramesh, Managing Director and co-founder of the Kairali Ayurvedic Group, is the bridge between the four generations who have dedicated their lives in promoting Ayurveda. He is blessed with the legacy of ancient science and is responsible for passing it on to the future generations. His contribution to Ayurveda, the alternative form of medicine, did not simply start from when he first founded the Kairali Ayurvedic Group through a small center in South Delhi. It can be traced to his teenage years when he used to lend a helping hand to his father Dr. K.S.V Vasudevan by mixing Ayurvedic medicine for patients. This not only proved to be an initial learning for him, but during those nascent years he also observed the way Ayurveda helped people. In addition, Mr. Ramesh is also the Director at Sumitomo Mitsui Construction Corporation Ltd.

She maintains an unfaltering commitment to the quality of knowledge, expertise, techniques and treatments used at Kairali’s retreats and spas, for which she has garnered praise and recognition as an industry pioneer.
