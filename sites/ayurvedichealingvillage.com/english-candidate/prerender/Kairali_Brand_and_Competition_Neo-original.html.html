Nearby Attractions and Current Events

Nearby Attractions and Current Events

The town of Palakkad lies at the foot of the Western Ghats, the dreamy land of misty mountains with its evergreen tropical forests, dams, wildlife sanctuaries, rivers, rare birds, historical monuments, temples and traditional Ayurvedic treatment centers. Palakkad is fast becoming a favourite destination for Kerala tour packages.

THIRUVALATHOOR

It is a peaceful village in Kerala's Palakkad district that is rich in culture and spirituality. It is home to the historic Thiruvalathoor Mahadeva Temple, a famous shrine to Lord Shiva. The temple, known for its beautiful wooden carvings and traditional Kerala-style architecture, attracts both devout and architecture aficionados. Thiruvalathoor, surrounded by thick greenery, has a serene ambiance that makes it an ideal location for spiritual searchers and nature enthusiasts.

Distance from Ayurvedic Healing Village: 10 km

KATHAKALI

Kathakali is an ancient classical dance drama that depicts an act of devotion and the universal conflict between good and evil. With years of pedagogy, the dance form uses choreography, face, and hand movements to express Indian folklore.

THENARI

Thenari is a lovely village in Kerala's Palakkad district that is well-known for its natural beauty and cultural significance. The town is famous for the Thenari Natural Spring, a sacred location with divine origins and ties to the Ramayana. Thenari, surrounded by rich greenery and quiet scenery, is a peaceful getaway for those seeking spiritual serenity and a connection with nature. Its tranquil surroundings and historical significance make it a hidden gem among Kerala's rich cultural legacy.

Distance from Ayurvedic Healing Village: 10 km

MOHINIYATTAM

The classical dance form of Kerala, Mohiniyattam, finds its roots in Hindu Mythology. The undulating movements depict the dance of the enchantress Mohini. She made demons fall for her oscillating dance steps, passionate eyes, and graceful gestures to restore elixir. A delight for your eyes, women adorn simple, elegant gold-filigreed dresses in pure white or ivory while performing.
