Attractions (25 to 50 Kms)

Although Palakkad is a small town, its development into a significant tourist destination is unsurprising given its rich culture, abundant greenery, and diverse flora and wildlife. There are magnificent and amazing spots near Palakkad that you will not regret visiting because the trip will be worthwhile. Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village provides trips that allow you to discover the magnificent beauty of Kerala.

Thiruvegappura Sankaranarayana Temple

This ancient 14th-century temple, also known as Moonnumoorthy temple, was built on the banks of Thootha, a tributary of the Bharathapuzha river. The temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva and Narayana. An elegant Koothambalam, or temple theatre, is claimed to have been erected on the structure around the 15th century.

Distance from Ayurvedic Healing Village: 25 Km

Meenvallam

The Meenvallam waterfalls, which originate at the mouth of the Thuppanadu River, are a hidden treasure in Kerala. This unique combination of mesmerizing beauty and panoramic splendor is a ten-step waterfall where the water drops from about 20 to 25 feet. The upper eight steps of the waterfall are hidden inside a lush forest, while the lower two are open to visitors.

Distance from Ayurvedic Healing Village: 30 Km

Meenkara

Meenkara is a tourist destination because of the dam built across the Meenkara River, a sub-tributary of Gayathripuzha that eventually merges into the Bharathapuzha River. The grandeur and pure beauty of this dam are a visual feast for spectators. It is an excellent picnic place, with a fish farm and lovely gardens.

Distance from Ayurvedic Healing Village: 32 Km

Kanjirapuzha Dam & Garden

Surrounded by the enthralling beauty of mountains, dense evergreen forests, and cotton clouds, Kanjirapuzha Dam is an alluring natural dam. The reservoir has a total of three islands within it and is composed of plenty of fish which is a sight to behold. If you are looking for peace amidst the divine mysticism of nature, you must visit this place once in Kerala.

Distance from Ayurvedic Healing Village: 40 Km

Pothundy

Constructed across the Meenichiladipuzha and Padipuzha rivers, Pothundy Dam is considered to be one of the oldest dams in India. The core wall of this dam is made with a mixture of jaggery and quick lime which makes it unique among others. Pothundy Dam is an ideal destination for those looking for picnics or a one-day trip. You can also visit nearby attractions such as Padagiri, Kilakettara, Nemmara, and Kollengode.

Distance from Ayurvedic Healing Village: 45 Km

Mangalam Dam

It is a renowned tourist destination and is located across the river Cherukunnath, a branch of the Mangalam River.

Distance from Ayurvedic Healing Village: 50 Km
