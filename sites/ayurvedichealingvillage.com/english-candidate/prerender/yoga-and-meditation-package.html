Yoga & Meditation Package

Yoga & Meditation Package

Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village provides comprehensive yoga and meditation packages that incorporate conventional Ayurvedic therapies along with daily yoga and meditation practices. Located in Palakkad Kerala, India, these packages feature expert-led yoga sessions, customized Ayurvedic consultations, organic dining, and accommodation in eco-friendly villas. The tranquil natural surroundings create a perfect atmosphere for healing, revitalization, and rediscovering oneself through time-honored wellness practices.

Your stay

Rates effective 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027. Per night, per villa, single or double occupancy; taxes included. Minimum seven nights. Deluxe Villa single: ₹21,490 × 7 nights = ₹150,430. Ref. KTAHV/YMT/EN-INR/20260925-R1.

Included

  • The villa in the category booked, and all government taxes
  • Three vegetarian or vegan meals a day, to the dietary prescription
  • One Ayurvedic therapy a day, including herbal steam where indicated
  • Daily consultation and monitoring by the attending physician
  • A private sunrise yoga session and a private guided meditation session each day
  • Weekly workshop, cooking demonstration and farm or village walk
  • Pool, library, gym and outdoor facilities; mineral water, herbal infused water and teas or juices after each session

Charged separately

  • Internal herbal medicines, laboratory tests and specialist therapies, prescribed as treatment progresses
  • Additional food beyond the prescribed meals
  • Laundry, telephone, internet and transport
  • Early check-in and late check-out, where granted

Booking and payment

  • A 40% deposit secures the booking; the balance is due no later than 30 days before admission
  • Requests for a specific villa must be made at least six months in advance and are subject to availability; confirmation requires full payment

Cancellation

  • More than 30 days before admission: full refund
  • 15 to 30 days before admission: 50% of the amount paid is refunded, less applicable transaction fees and taxes
  • Fewer than 15 days before admission, or a change of dates during the stay: no refund

Where we are

Kairali sits in Palakkad district, inland Kerala, in the south west of India.
