About the Program

Your gut is the key to well-being. The digestive wellness yoga program at Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village is a dedicated retreat aimed at improving gut health, harmonizing digestive fire (Agni), and fostering overall wellness through a holistic blend of yoga therapy, Ayurvedic treatments, and sattvic nutrition. This ayurvedic gut health therapy integrates carefully selected asanas, pranayama, and pranavmukthasna kriya to stimulate the digestive organs, and basic dietary recommendations to stimulate Agni (digestive fire), alleviate bloating, and promote gut harmony. Every session is guided by knowledgeable instructors who concentrate on posture, breathing, and intentional movement. Perfect for individuals facing issues like acidity, constipation, IBS, or slow digestion, the holistic Ayurvedic program for Gut issues encourages natural recovery, enhanced metabolism, and general wellness. Appropriate for every level, it’s a soothing yet impactful path to digestive balance.

Process Involved in Digestive Wellness & Gut Health Yoga Therapy

Digestive Wellness & Gut Health Yoga Therapy at Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village includes the following steps:

01

Consultation: We begin with a thorough consultation to understand your medical history, symptoms, and lifestyle. This helps us determine your unique dosha imbalance and design a personalized treatment plan.

02

Diagnosis: Through pulse examination (Nadi Pariksha) and visual examination (Darshana Pariksha), we assess the dosha imbalance and identify the root cause of your condition.

03

Treatment Plan: Based on the diagnosis, we create a comprehensive treatment plan that includes a combination of therapies, herbal medications, dietary recommendations, and lifestyle modifications.

04

Ayurvedic Therapies: Our therapies include abhyangam, dharas, podikizhi, navarakizhi, virechanam, matravasti,kashayavasti, etc, based on the body type and dosha balance.

05

Private Yoga Sessions: Exclusive 2-hour personalized sessions (Theory + Practical) of Asanas, Pranayama, Pavanamukthasana Kriya, and Agnisara Kriya with Pranayama to enhance digestion, stimulate agni (digestive fire), and restore gut harmony.

06

Herbal Medications: Doctors prescribe Ayurvedic medications made from natural herbs to support the internal healing process and strengthen the body.

07

Dietary and Lifestyle Guidance: Ayurvedic doctors will provide dietary recommendations tailored to your dosha imbalance and suggest lifestyle modifications to maintain balance and prevent further aggravation of symptoms.

08

Follow-up and Monitoring: We schedule regular follow-up sessions to track your progress, make necessary adjustments to the treatment plan, and provide ongoing support and guidance.

FAQs

The World Health Organization reports traditional medicine in use across more than 170 of its 194 member states. Here are the questions guests most often ask before starting the digestive wellness & gut health programme.

What is Digestive Wellness & Gut Health Yoga Therapy at Kairali?

Digestive Wellness & Gut Health Yoga Therapy unites Ayurveda and Yoga to enhance digestion and stabilize gut health. It encompasses tailored therapies, natural remedies, and therapeutic yoga, such as Pawanmukhtasana Kriya and Kapalabhati. Effective for issues such as bloating, acidity, and IBS, it rejuvenates the digestive fire (Agni) and enhances overall vitality.

Which yoga practices are included in the gut health program?

The therapy includes a combination of Asanas like Pranayama, Pavanamukthasana Kriya, and Agnisara Kriya, along with Pranayama techniques such as Kapalabhati and Nadi Shodhana. These practices are carefully selected to aid digestion, support liver function, and calm the nervous system, enhancing overall gut health.

Can this program help with chronic digestive issues like IBS, bloating, or acidity?

Yes. At Kairali, we tackle the underlying issue through Ayurvedic diagnosis (Dosha assessment) and customize the treatment accordingly. In conjunction with yoga and herbal remedies, this program provides substantial relief for issues like Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS), constipation, gas, acidity, and bloating, fostering long-term digestive wellness.

Is the gut health therapy suitable for all body types (Doshas)?

The program is tailored following an initial consultation with our Ayurvedic experts. Regardless of whether you are Vata, Pitta, or Kapha predominant, the yoga treatment and dietary plan will be customized to harmonize your unique Dosha and digestive characteristics.

How long should I stay at the Ayurvedic Healing Village to see results?

Our doctors suggest at least 14 days for significant improvement in digestive health. For long-term problems, a 21-day program offers enhanced detoxification and gut healing with ongoing expert supervision and daily Yoga.
