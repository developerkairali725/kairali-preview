Around Palakkad & beyond

Sightseeing near Kairali

Discover the temples, riverside landscapes and forested hills around us. Choose from 22 places to visit, with approximate road distances from The Ayurvedic Healing Village to help you plan.

Planning an outing during your stay? Speak to reception about transport and fitting it around your appointments.

01 / Explore

Heritage & culture

Temples, historic streets and Kerala’s living arts.

Thiruvalathoor Temple

Approx. 6 kmby road from Kairali

An ancient Bhagavathy temple known for its woodwork and stone sculpture. Local tradition tells of a surrounding wall left unfinished before daybreak.

Distance to: Temple approach.

Open directions ↗

Chittur Gurumadam

Approx. 7 kmby road from Kairali

A riverside memorial to Thunchath Ezhuthachan, whose Malayalam retellings of the Ramayana and Mahabharata shaped the language’s literary tradition. Manuscripts and objects associated with the poet are part of its heritage.

Distance to: Gurumadam approach.

Open directions ↗

Thenari Sree Rama Temple

Approx. 9 kmby road from Kairali

A place of worship associated with the Ramayana, with a sacred spring beside the temple. Its water is revered in local tradition.

Distance to: Sree Rama Theertham approach.

Open directions ↗

Palakkad Fort · Tipu’s Fort

Approx. 10 kmby road from Kairali

Built by Hyder Ali in 1766, this protected fort is one of Palakkad’s best-known landmarks. Its moat, ramparts and grounds offer a view of the town’s history; the Anjaneya shrine stands inside.

Distance to: Fort road approach; walk to entrance.

Open directions ↗

Jainimedu Jain Temple

Approx. 14 kmby road from Kairali

A historic granite temple dedicated to Chandraprabha, with images of Jain Tirthankaras. The surrounding neighbourhood is also associated with poet Kumaran Asan and Veenapoovu.

Distance to: Temple approach.

Open directions ↗

Kalpathy Shiva Temple

Approx. 16 kmby road from Kairali

Visit the Vishwanatha Swamy temple and the surrounding agraharam streets. Kalpathy is especially known for its annual chariot festival, Ratholsavam.

Distance to: Vishwanatha Swamy temple.

Open directions ↗

Kerala Kalamandalam

Approx. 65 kmby road from Kairali

At Cheruthuruthy, this renowned institution preserves Kerala’s traditional performing arts. Arrange a visitor programme in advance if you hope to see teaching or performances.

Distance to: Campus road approach.

Open directions ↗

Thiruvegappura Sankaranarayana Temple

Approx. 80 kmby road from Kairali

Also known as the Moonnumoorthy temple, this riverside temple is associated with Shiva, Vishnu and Sankaranarayana. Its traditional koothambalam, or temple theatre, is an important architectural feature.

Distance to: Temple approach.

Open directions ↗

Thrithala

Approx. 80 kmby road from Kairali

Explore a village on the Bharathapuzha known for its temple traditions and the historic Kattil Madom monument nearby. Allow additional travel for individual sites around the village.

Distance to: Thrithala village centre.

Open directions ↗

Guruvayur

Approx. 100 kmby road from Kairali

The Sree Krishna temple is one of Kerala’s important pilgrimage destinations, known for devotional offerings such as Thulabharam. Check temple entry rules and arrangements before travelling.

Distance to: Road approach near Sree Krishna temple.

Open directions ↗

02 / Explore

Gardens, dams & waterfalls

Waterside views, sculpture and places to explore around Palakkad.

Fantasy Park

Approx. 19 kmby road from Kairali

An amusement and water park near Malampuzha, with activities for families. Check the park’s current opening schedule, available rides and entry requirements before visiting.

Distance to: Park entrance approach.

Open directions ↗

Malampuzha Rock Garden

Approx. 19 kmby road from Kairali

Designed by Nek Chand, the garden turns discarded tiles, bangles, stones and other materials into sculpture. A distinctive art stop in the Malampuzha area.

Distance to: Rock Garden approach.

Kerala Tourism currently reports the main Malampuzha Garden closed, with the dam and ropeway open. Check the latest notice and access to individual attractions before setting out.

Open directions ↗Latest visitor information ↗

Malampuzha Dam & Gardens

Approx. 20 kmby road from Kairali

The reservoir, dam and gardens sit below the surrounding hills. The area is also known for Kanayi Kunhiraman’s Yakshi sculpture and the aerial ropeway.

Distance to: Dam approach.

Kerala Tourism currently reports the main Malampuzha Garden closed, with the dam and ropeway open. Check the latest notice and access to individual attractions before setting out.

Open directions ↗Latest visitor information ↗

Meenkara Dam

Approx. 20 kmby road from Kairali

A reservoir set in the countryside near the Kerala–Tamil Nadu border. The dam and surrounding landscape make a quieter stop for views of the water and hills.

Distance to: Dam road approach.

Open directions ↗

Pothundy Dam

Approx. 35 kmby road from Kairali

An old earthen dam near Nenmara, with reservoir views and a backdrop of the Nelliyampathy hills. It can be a stop on the route towards Nelliyampathy.

Distance to: Dam approach.

Open directions ↗

Meenvallam Waterfalls

Approx. 40 kmby road from Kairali

A forest waterfall on the Thuppanadu river in the Kalladikodan hills. Access involves walking beyond the road approach; check seasonal restrictions and local entry arrangements.

Distance to: Nearest mapped road; final walk extra.

Open directions ↗Latest visitor information ↗

Mangalam Dam

Approx. 45 kmby road from Kairali

A reservoir bordered by wooded hills and countryside. A place to take in the landscape, with access to visitor areas subject to local conditions.

Distance to: Dam approach.

Open directions ↗

Kanjirapuzha Dam & Garden

Approx. 45 kmby road from Kairali

A broad reservoir framed by hills and forest, with a garden near the dam. Its wooded surroundings and islands form the main attraction.

Distance to: Visitor garden approach.

Open directions ↗

03 / Explore

Hills & wildlife

Longer outings into the Western Ghats, with entry arrangements to check before you go.

Nelliyampathy

Approx. 55 kmby road from Kairali

A hill area of plantations, wooded slopes and viewpoints reached by a winding mountain road. The estimate is to Nelliyampathy village; individual viewpoints and estates are farther on.

Distance to: Nelliyampathy village.

Open directions ↗

Silent Valley National Park

Approx. 65 kmby road from Kairali

A protected rainforest landscape in the Western Ghats. Plan entry through Mukkali with the forest department; organised park transport and entry arrangements are separate from the drive to Mukkali.

Distance to: Mukkali; park transport extra.

Open directions ↗Latest visitor information ↗

Parambikulam Tiger Reserve

Approx. 65 kmby road from Kairali

A protected forest landscape in the Anamalai hills, home to diverse wildlife. Contact the reserve about permitted activities, reservations and entry times; wildlife sightings are never guaranteed.

Distance to: Anappady information centre approach.

Open directions ↗Latest visitor information ↗

Attappadi

Approx. 70 kmby road from Kairali

A wide mountain valley around the upper Bhavani river, beneath the Nilgiri hills. The estimate is to Agali, a practical starting point for the area; onward travel depends on your destination.

Distance to: Agali road approach.

Open directions ↗

Before you go

A little planning makes the day easier

Distances are approximate, one-way road estimates from Kairali in Olassery, checked on 24 September 2026. They are rounded and may vary with your route, traffic restrictions and entrance used. Walking, park transport and travel within larger destinations are additional.

Confirm opening times, tickets and permissions before travelling. Temple entry and dress requirements vary. Forest visits may require advance booking, and weather can affect access to waterfalls and mountain roads.
