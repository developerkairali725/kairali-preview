The Practice

Yoga & Meditation at the Healing Village

Yoga and meditation are woven into every day at Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village. Mornings begin with sunrise group yoga and pranayama; evenings close with guided meditation — practised across 65 lush acres at the foothills of the Western Ghats in Palakkad, Kerala.

Every programme at the NABH-accredited Healing Village includes daily group yoga and meditation sessions, guided by experienced teachers and supervised by Ayurvedic physicians. The five programmes below deepen that practice — from therapeutic yoga for digestion and stress to residential retreats and teacher training.

A Day of Practice

Four moments from the daily routine (Dinacharya) every residential programme follows.

06:30

Practice Yoga

Practice yoga asanas (postures) for 30-45 minutes to stimulate the body and promote flexibility.

07:15

Pranayama

Engage in pranayama (breathing exercises) and meditation for mental clarity and relaxation.

17:00

Yoga Practice

Practice gentle yoga or pranayama to further enhance the body-mind connection and relieve any accumulated stress or tension.

18:00

Meditation Session

Participate in a guided meditation or mindfulness session to calm the mind and promote emotional balance.
