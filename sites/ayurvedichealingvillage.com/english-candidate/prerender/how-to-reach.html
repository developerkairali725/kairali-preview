Arriving from outside India

Fly to Coimbatore (CJB)

The nearest airport — a private car transfer of about 1½–2 hours. Kochi / Cochin (COK) is a good alternative when it has better connections from your country — about 2½–3 hours by road.

The nearest railhead, about 35 minutes away — or drive in on the NH544 Coimbatore–Kochi corridor.

Your arrival in India

Your arrival, step by step

  1. Clear immigration and collect your baggage.
  2. Exit through the arrivals gate.
  3. A Kairali representative receives you with a name-board. You receive the driver’s name and vehicle details before you land.

  5. If you cannot find the driver, call or message the arrival number and wait inside the terminal.

Before you travel

Frequently asked questions

Should I fly to Coimbatore or Kochi?

Coimbatore (CJB) is the nearest airport, about one and a half to two hours by road, and the simplest arrival for most visitors. Choose Kochi / Cochin (COK) — about two and a half to three hours by road — when it offers a better international connection from your country.

How far is the nearest airport?

Coimbatore International Airport (CJB) is the nearest at around 62 km — about one and a half to two hours by car. Kochi / Cochin International Airport (COK) is around 115 km away, about two and a half to three hours by road.

Do you arrange pick-up from the airport or railway station?

Yes. The hospital can arrange a private car for arriving patients. Message our patient care team on WhatsApp, or call the main line, and share your arrival date, time and flight or train number. A team member will confirm the driver and vehicle details.

How will I recognise the driver?

A Kairali representative waits for you in the arrivals area with a name-board. You receive the driver’s name and vehicle details before you land, so you know exactly who is picking you up.

Is pick-up available for late-evening arrivals?

Yes. A pre-arranged transfer is recommended for late arrivals, when local roads are quiet. Share your flight details in advance and your driver will wait for you at any hour.

Can I come by train?

Yes. Palakkad Junction (PGT) is the recommended station, around 18 km away — about a 35-minute drive. Taxis are available at the station, or send us your train number and arrival time and we will pick you up.

Is the trip safe for a solo traveller?

Kerala is among the calmest regions of India, and English is widely spoken. With a pre-arranged transfer you are met by a named driver, so there is no need to look for a taxi on arrival. Many of our patients travel to us alone.

Can you accommodate a wheelchair or a caregiver?

Yes. A wheelchair-accessible vehicle and an accompanying caregiver can be arranged. Please tell us your requirements at booking so the right vehicle is ready.

Do I need an Indian visa?

Most international visitors need a valid passport and an Indian visa or an eligible e-Visa. Requirements vary by nationality and can change — please check the official Government of India visa portal before you travel.

Is this a hospital or a spa?

Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village is an NABH-accredited Ayurvedic hospital. Care is led by registered Ayurvedic physicians, and every treatment programme begins with a clinical consultation. It is not a spa.
