Our Approach Spinal Disc & Nerve Restoration Treatment

With over 118 years of Ayurvedic expertise, Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village offers doctor-guided Ayurvedic programmes for spinal-disc and nerve-related concerns, tailored after individual assessment. Ayurveda, an ancient holistic system, restores balance and harmony to combat slip disc. It views slip disc as a Vata dosha imbalance affecting movement and the nervous system. Our approach combines therapies like Abhyanga (therapeutic oil massage), Pizhichil (oil bath), Kati Basti (herbal oil retention on the affected area), and Greeva Basti (neck treatment) to ease pain, reduce inflammation, and nourish spinal discs. Herbal Ayurvedic medications strengthen the spine, enhance flexibility, and promote tissue regeneration. These remedies work together to heal the slip disc, relieve pain, and prevent recurrence. Ayurveda emphasizes lifestyle changes: a balanced diet, yoga, meditation, and gentle exercises for healing and long-term well-being. Opt for Ayurveda for personalized care, natural healing, and a holistic approach addressing the root cause of slip disc. Trust Kairali’s extensive experience for effective and safe treatment.

Process Involved in Treatment for Spinal Disc & Nerve Restoration

At our wellness retreat, we follow a comprehensive process for treating slip disc, combining Ayurvedic therapies and personalized care:

01

Consultation: We begin with a detailed consultation to understand your medical history, symptoms, and lifestyle factors contributing to slip disc.

02

Diagnosis: Our experienced Ayurvedic doctor will perform a thorough examination to determine the specific imbalances and severity of the slip disc.

03

Treatment Plan: Based on the diagnosis, a personalized treatment plan is created, incorporating a range of Ayurvedic therapies and medications tailored to your needs.

04

Ayurvedic Therapies: You will undergo various therapeutic treatments such as Abhyanga (therapeutic oil massage), Pizhichil (oil bath), Kati Basti (herbal oil retention on the affected area), and Greeva Basti (neck treatment) to alleviate pain, reduce inflammation, and nourish the affected spinal discs.

05

Herbal Medications: Our Ayurvedic doctor will prescribe herbal formulations to strengthen the spine, improve flexibility, and promote tissue regeneration.

06

Lifestyle Modifications: We guide you in adopting a balanced diet, incorporating specific exercises, practicing yoga, and meditation to support the healing process and prevent slip disc recurrence.

07

Ongoing Support: Throughout your stay, our team will provide continuous support, monitor your progress, and make any necessary adjustments to your treatment plan.

08

Education and Guidance: We empower you with knowledge about self-care practices, postural corrections, and preventive measures to maintain a healthy spine even after leaving the retreat.

FAQs

The World Health Organization reports traditional medicine in use across more than 170 of its 194 member states. Here are the questions guests most often ask before starting the spinal disc and nerve restoration programme.

What is slip disc?

Slip disc, also known as a herniated disc, occurs when the soft cushioning disc between the spinal vertebrae ruptures or bulges, causing pain, numbness, and tingling sensations.

How can Ayurveda help with slip disc?

Ayurveda offers a holistic approach that focuses on balancing the body and addressing the root cause of slip disc, providing natural pain relief, reducing inflammation, and promoting tissue regeneration.

Is Ayurvedic treatment safe for slip disc?

Yes, Ayurvedic Back Disc Treatment is generally safe as it utilizes natural therapies and herbal medications. However, it is important to consult with a qualified Ayurvedic doctor and disclose any underlying health conditions or medications you are taking.
