Planning your admission and follow-up

Preparing for admission

Start with Priya, the sales team or a free video, phone or email consultation. A coordinator will arrange the consultation after your request. Reports may be shared by upload where available or by email to info@kairali.com. Reports and pre-admission clearance are required only for severe conditions, case by case.

Planning assistance during your stay

Let the team know about mobility or care needs before admission. Routine help with walking or using a wheelchair between your room and the treatment centre is included. A dedicated nurse or attendant can be arranged at an additional cost according to the support required. If you use CPAP or oxygen, preferably bring your own equipment; hospital arrangements require the doctor’s approval and information at least one week in advance.

After discharge

Four follow-up consultations within one year are included. You schedule each consultation with the doctor; times are not fixed when you leave. Contact the team when you need help arranging a follow-up.

Understanding Ayurveda

4 questions
↑ Back to topics
+

What is Ayurveda, and how can it benefit me?

Ayurveda is a classical Indian system of medicine whose texts date back over two thousand years. It focuses on balance between body, mind and spirit through personalised therapies, nutrition, herbal treatments and lifestyle practices, and aims to address the underlying causes of imbalance rather than only symptoms.

+

How can I incorporate Ayurvedic principles into my daily life?

  • Wake up before sunrise and sleep by 10 PM
  • Practice mindful eating with fresh, seasonal foods
  • Stay hydrated with warm water throughout the day
  • Incorporate gentle movement like yoga or walking
  • Practice oil pulling and self-massage (abhyanga) with appropriate oils
  • Adjust your diet according to your dominant dosha (body constitution)

Our physicians provide personalised recommendations during your stay.

+

What are doshas, and why are they important?

Doshas are the three bodily energies—Vata (air and space), Pitta (fire and water), and Kapha (earth and water)—that govern all physical and mental processes. Understanding your unique dosha composition helps determine the most effective treatments, diet, and lifestyle practices for your constitution. At Kairali, we conduct a thorough dosha assessment upon arrival to customize your wellness program.

+

Are Ayurvedic doctors real doctors?

Yes. Ayurvedic physicians in India complete a five-and-a-half-year BAMS (Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery) degree that includes clinical training in Ayurvedic and modern medical practice. Many pursue further specialisation (MD in Ayurveda) over an additional three years. Ayurvedic medicine is recognised by the WHO and regulated in India by the Ministry of AYUSH.

Read more on our Knowledge Centre.

Treatments & Programs

+

Which health conditions do your programmes support?

Whether a programme suits you depends on your individual assessment, and Ayurvedic care is offered alongside, not instead of, the treatment you already receive. Our physicians see patients with concerns including:

  • Digestive disorders (IBS, acid reflux, constipation)
  • Respiratory conditions (asthma, allergies, sinusitis)
  • Joint and muscular pain (arthritis, back pain)
  • Stress-related disorders (anxiety, insomnia, fatigue)
  • Skin conditions (psoriasis, eczema, acne)
  • Metabolic disorders (diabetes, obesity, thyroid imbalances)
  • Women’s health issues (PCOS, menopause, hormonal imbalances)
  • Neurological conditions (migraines, nerve support therapy)
+

What treatments and programmes do you offer at Kairali?

Kairali offers Ayurvedic programmes tailored to your constitution and health goals, each beginning at a physician-set minimum stay:

  • Panchakarma & Detoxification — from 14 nights
  • Stress Relief & Mental Wellness — from 7 nights
  • Chronic condition support (arthritis, diabetes, hypertension, respiratory, neurological) — from 7 to 14 nights depending on the condition
  • Weight Management & Metabolism — from 14 nights
  • Women's Hormonal Balance & Endocrine Therapy — from 21 nights; Postpartum Rejuvenation — from 14 nights
  • Musculoskeletal Care (back, neck, joints, spine) — from 14 nights
  • Skin & Chronic Skin Conditions — from 14 nights
  • Immunity Enhancement — from 7 nights

Each programme includes daily prescribed therapies, a personalised diet, and yoga and meditation sessions. Internal herbal medicines, lab tests and specialty therapies are charged as needed.

See full programme details on our programmes page.

+

How long should I stay for effective results?

Recommended stay length depends on your programme and is set by the physician at consultation:

  • 3 nights — the Preventive Health & Regenerative programme, an introduction to Ayurveda
  • 7 nights — stress relief, sleep, immunity and several other programmes
  • 14 nights — Panchakarma, musculoskeletal, skin, weight-management and most chronic-condition programmes
  • 21 nights — hormonal/endocrine and addiction-recovery programmes

Our physicians confirm the exact duration for your condition at consultation.

+

Will I experience any side effects during treatment?

Some patients notice mild, temporary effects during the first few days, such as headaches, tiredness or changes in digestion. Tell your physician or nurse about any new or worsening symptom; they monitor your progress closely and adjust your plan where needed.

+

Is Panchakarma uncomfortable, and what does a treatment day feel like?

Most programmes include two Ayurvedic therapies a day (one a day for the Preventive Health & Regenerative programme), alongside sunrise yoga and evening meditation. Some patients notice mild detoxification symptoms such as headaches, fatigue or digestive changes in the first few days — tell your care team about any symptoms so your physician can review them. Your physician adjusts your plan for comfort throughout.

+

Can I work remotely during my treatment stay?

Remote work is possible with your physician’s permission. Agree the time allowed with the physician so that work fits around treatment, rest and your individual care plan.

Weather & Seasonal Information

2 questions
↑ Back to topics
+

What is the climate like at Kairali throughout the year?

Kerala has a warm, tropical climate with four seasons:

  • Summer (March–May) — 28–38°C; warm, sunny days, good for pitta-pacifying treatments
  • Monsoon (June–September) — 23–32°C; regular rainfall, the traditional season for intensive Panchakarma
  • Post-monsoon/Autumn (October–November) — 24–32°C; pleasant, good for rejuvenation therapies
  • Winter (December–February) — 20–30°C; mild days and cooler evenings, good for vata-balancing therapies
+

When is the best time to visit for specific treatments?

While treatments are beneficial year-round, monsoon (June–September) is traditionally best for intensive Panchakarma and detoxification, winter (December–February) suits rejuvenation and immunity programmes, and summer (March–May) suits pitta-pacifying and stress-management treatments. Our physicians advise on the best timing for your condition.

Insurance & Payment

6 questions
↑ Back to topics
+

Are Ayurvedic treatments covered by insurance?

Kairali is not currently empanelled with Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY), CGHS or other government schemes, and we do not offer cashless tie-ups with private insurers. Many patients claim reimbursement afterwards by submitting our invoices and medical documents to their insurer.

We can provide detailed invoices, treatment summaries, prescriptions and admission/discharge notes to support a reimbursement claim, and can guide you on the documents insurers typically ask for. Final approval always depends on your insurer's policy.

+

Can you help with insurance documentation?

Yes. The patient care team provides detailed invoices, treatment summaries and medical certificates that meet the requirements of Indian and international insurers. Let us know your insurance needs when booking so we can prepare the right documentation.

+

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), bank transfers, cash (Indian Rupees, Euros or US Dollars) and UPI payments for Indian residents.

+

Can I pay in US dollars or euros, and is GST included in the price?

Yes. Our published rates are quoted in INR, USD and EUR, cash is accepted in Indian Rupees, Euros or US Dollars, and government taxes are included in the published programme and villa fees. Airport/station transfer rates separately state 'inclusive of 5% GST'.

+

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

Standard cancellations: more than 30 days before admission, full refund; 15 to 30 days before admission, 50% of the amount paid is refunded, less applicable transaction fees and taxes; fewer than 15 days before admission, or a change of dates during the stay, no refund. Clinical unsuitability and visa refusal are handled under the specific policies explained below. Contact the team to confirm the applicable arrangement.

See our Cancellation & Refund Policy page.

+

Do you offer any discounts (senior citizens, defence personnel, longer stays)?

Yes, on direct bookings: 5% off for booking 90 days in advance, 5% seasonal discount (April–September), 7% returning-patient discount (10% after five previous stays), and 5% off stays longer than 28 nights. Please ask the patient care team about any other concession.

What special considerations do you have for senior patients?

Routine assistance, such as help walking or travelling by wheelchair between your room and the treatment centre, is available without requiring a dedicated attendant. If you need dedicated nursing or attendant support, this can be arranged at an additional cost based on the level of assistance required. Can you accommodate extended stays for international patients?

Can you arrange a taxi from Coimbatore airport late at night?

Yes, a private transfer from Coimbatore Airport is available at ₹6,775 (MPV) or ₹23,711 (Sedan, premium), one way, valid 1 Oct 2026 – 30 Sep 2027, inclusive of 5% GST. Please confirm your arrival time with the patient care team when booking.

Do you provide an invitation letter for a medical visa, and what does it contain?

Can you arrange language support for consultations?
