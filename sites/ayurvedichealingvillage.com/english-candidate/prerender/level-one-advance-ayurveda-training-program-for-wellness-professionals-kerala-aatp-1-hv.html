Level One Advanced Ayurveda Training Program

A ten-day residential programme for wellness professionals: two hours of theory and two hours of practical each day, building from the foundations of Ayurveda to the confident performance of Abhyangam, Shirodhara, Nasyam, Podikizhi, Udwarthanam and Kativasti, and to basic Ayurvedic wellness consultations. Certification on successful completion.

The programme

Process Involved In the Level One Advanced Program

Ten days, each pairing a theory block with a practical block. Therapies are learnt in stages — head, neck and body, then the prone position, then full body — before the next therapy is introduced.

01

Day 1 — Introduction to Ayurveda, its history, branches and divisions; Abhyangam of the head, neck and body.

02

Day 2 — Panchamahabhuta, Tridosha, Saptadhatu, Agni and Ama; Abhyangam in the prone position.

03

Day 3 — Swastha Lakshana, the Trigunas, health and disease; full-body Abhyangam.

04

Day 4 — Treatment modalities and Shirodhara theory; Shirodhara practical.

05

Day 5 — Abhyangam and Shirodhara review, Nasyam theory; Nasyam practical.

06

Day 6 — Dosha, Dhatu and Mala, the classification of disease, Podikizhi theory; Podikizhi preparation, tying and application.

07

Day 7 — Dinacharya, Ritucharya, anatomy and physiology; Podikizhi practical.

08

Day 8 — Ahara and Vihara, the basics of Dravyaguna, anatomy part two; Udwarthanam theory and practical.

09

Day 9 — Roga and Rogi Pareeksha, an introduction to Panchakarma, Kativasti theory; Kativasti practical.

10

Day 10 — Prakriti basics and the analysis questionnaire; review of all therapies and closing Q&A.

Features of the program

  • ◆Format: forty hours — two hours of theory and two hours of practical daily over ten days — with group yoga and meditation sessions.
  • ◆Who it suits: therapists, wellness counsellors and the wider wellness fraternity; yoga instructors and holistic practitioners.
  • ◆Included: villa stay with ensuite amenities, all vegetarian meals, daily herbal water, group yoga and meditation, lectures and demonstrations, a village walk, lifestyle and fitness evaluations, and use of the pool, gym and library. Additional food, individual sessions, factory visits and internal medicines are charged separately.
  • ◆Stay: ten nights in the hospital's villas, charged per night under the training tariff — Deluxe Villa ₹18,410 single / ₹30,530 double ($263 / $436; €230 / €382).

Learning outcomes

  • ◆Six therapies: perform Abhyangam, Shirodhara, Nasyam, Podikizhi, Udwarthanam and Kativasti to the hospital's standard.
  • ◆Consultation: conduct a basic Ayurvedic wellness consultation, including Prakriti analysis.
  • ◆Foundations: explain Ayurvedic anatomy and physiology, the daily routine (Dinacharya) and the seasonal regimen (Ritucharya).
  • ◆Diet and regimen: advise on Ahara and Vihara from Ayurvedic principles.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need prior Ayurveda training?

The programme is designed for people already working in wellness or therapy. It begins from the foundations of Ayurveda, so formal prior study is not required.

Can the schedule be adapted?

Within the ten-day structure the schedule can be adjusted to a candidate's needs; discuss this at enquiry.

Is there a certificate?

Yes. Certification is awarded on successful completion.

What is included in the stay?

Villa accommodation, all vegetarian meals, daily herbal water, group yoga and meditation, lectures, demonstrations and a village walk, lifestyle and fitness evaluations, and the pool, gym and library. Additional food, individual sessions, factory visits and internal medicines are charged separately.

What comes after Level One?

The twenty-day Advanced Ayurveda Lifestyle Consultant & Therapist Training, Kairali's practitioner certification.
