Common questions

Is Kairali a hospital or a spa?◆

Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village is an NABH-accredited Ayurvedic hospital. Care is led by registered Ayurvedic physicians, and every treatment programme follows a clinical consultation. It is not a spa.

Which number should current or returning patients use?◆

Message our patient care coordinator on WhatsApp at +91 92898 38797, or call our main line at +91 9555 156 156. Share your patient reference so we can route you to the right physician.

Can I speak with a physician before I travel?◆

Yes. A complimentary online consultation with a registered Ayurvedic physician lets us assess your suitability and recommend a programme before you arrive.

Where exactly are you, and how do I get there?◆

We’re at III/73 Palayamkadu, P.O. Olassery, Kodumbu, Palakkad District 678551, Kerala. The nearest airport is Coimbatore (about an hour away) and Palakkad Junction is the nearest major railway station. Our How to Reach page has directions, and we can arrange a pickup.

What are your timings?◆

Our phone line is answered from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST, every day. Our patient care team usually replies to WhatsApp and email messages within working hours, and on the next morning otherwise.

Do you work with travel agencies and partners?◆

Yes — including specialist operators across India, the DACH region and France. Write to info@kairali.com and our partnerships desk will be in touch.
