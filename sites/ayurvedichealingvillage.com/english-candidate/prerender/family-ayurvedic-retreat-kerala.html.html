About us

Wellness for Every Generation – Family Ayurveda Escape in Kerala

Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village is one of the best Ayurvedic retreats in Kerala, India, offering families a unique space to relax, heal, and reconnect. With over 35 years of expertise, we combine authentic Ayurvedic traditions with modern comforts, creating the perfect Kerala Ayurveda retreat for family vacations.

Our 65-acre retreat brings wellness to every generation of your family. While you enjoy rejuvenating therapies and personalized treatments, your elderly parents can find gentle care and holistic support tailored to their needs. It's more than a break—it's a multigenerational Ayurveda retreat where adult children and their parents come together to restore balance and harmony.

From personalized Panchakarma programs to family-oriented wellness consultations, nature walks, and wholesome sattvic cuisine, every detail is crafted to ensure your family experiences the best of Ayurveda and togetherness across generations.
