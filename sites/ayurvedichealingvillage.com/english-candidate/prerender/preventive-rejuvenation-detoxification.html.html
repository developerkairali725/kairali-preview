Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Detoxification important for the human body?

Detoxification is important for the human body because it helps to eliminate harmful toxins and chemicals that accumulate in the body over time. These toxins can contribute to a variety of health issues and can impair the body's natural ability to function optimally.

Why should you opt for the treatment?

The human body tirelessly processes various substances and differentiates between what's beneficial and what's harmful. However, it's now overburdened with toxins, stress, and processed foods, causing a buildup of toxicity in the system. A detox and rejuvenation program can help unload the stress and cleanse the body for better health.

What would be the duration of the treatment?

You can opt for this program for a minimum of 7 days up to 14 days depending upon your consultation with the doctor

Is any specific or generic post-treatment care required?

Post-care or "Paschat Karma" is as important as the main detox procedures. It acts as a buffer period for your digestive system, allowing you to gradually reintroduce simple foods and eventually your regular healthy diet. During this period, your body continues to process the toxins that were released during the detox and your digestive system becomes more sensitive to overly stimulating or processed foods. Our practitioners will design a balanced and appropriate diet to help you make a slow and smooth transition back to your normal routine

What are the outcomes of the treatment?

The Ayurvedic detox and rejuvenation program purifies and rests the digestive system to improve the strength of Agni, the metabolic fire, throughout the body. This process fosters a sense of energy, vitality, and enthusiasm for life, and supports healthy weight and overall health. The program provides an opportunity for self-reflection and deeper inner awareness, and a healthy diet and lifestyle can further enhance your well-being even after the program is complete.
