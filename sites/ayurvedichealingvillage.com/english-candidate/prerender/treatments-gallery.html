Treatment rooms and preparation

10 photographs

Treatment rooms are brick-walled, with a carved wooden therapy table, a hanging brass dhara vessel and candles. Before a session the therapists prepare the kizhi boluses, herbal pastes and warm oils in brass vessels; a physician's pulse examination and the changing area are also shown.

Abhyanga and oil therapies

9 photographs

Abhyanga is the synchronised application of warm herbal oil by two therapists, here along the legs, feet, shoulders and scalp. Pizhichil pours the oil from a brass vessel over the body; Udwarthanam works herbal powder into the skin instead of oil.

Shirodhara and Thakradhara

7 photographs

In Shirodhara a continuous stream of warm herbal oil falls from a suspended brass vessel onto the forehead; Thakradhara uses medicated buttermilk in the same way. The patient lies on the wooden table with cotton eye pads while a therapist steadies the vessel.

◆Read about the treatments→

Vasti and Pichu

4 photographs

Janu Vasti holds warm medicated oil in a ring of herbal dough sealed around the knee. Greeva Pichu and Shiro Pichu place an oil-soaked cotton pad on the upper spine or the crown and pour warm oil over it from a brass vessel.

◆Read about the treatments→

Kizhi, Swedana and Thalapothichil

9 photographs

Pinda Sweda presses warm herbal boluses (kizhi) over the back, chest and limbs; Bashpa Swedana is herbal steam taken seated in a wooden chamber with only the head outside. In Thalapothichil the scalp is covered with a medicated herbal paste.
