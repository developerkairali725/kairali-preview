Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a one-size-fits-all approach ?

Our retreat features a particular health philosophy, everyone's body and fitness level are unique. Kairali - The Ayurvedic Healing Village adapts to your needs, no matter where you are on your weight loss journey.

Will the retreat program adapt to my injuries or physical limitations?

Ayurvedic Physicians at Kairali - The Ayurvedic Healing Village will offer a program that meets your physical abilities. An established weight loss program will feature yoga, meditation, diet & exercise as appropriate for you with a limited range of motion, chronic injuries, or other unique considerations.

Will the program accommodate my dietary restrictions?

When choosing a weight loss program, a Customized diet will be recommended based on your taste and lifestyle. Kairali - The Ayurvedic Healing Village’s dietitian will create a personalized nutrition plan that considers vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and other special dietary needs. The advice and education you receive should result in your ability to replicate healthy, nutritious meals successfully at home.

Is there a mental health aspect to the program?

Yes, achieving a balance between physical and emotional well-being is essential for your ongoing success in your weight loss journey. At Kairali - The Ayurvedic Healing Village, our experienced physicians & yoga trainers provide you the support for emotional well-being and balancing your mind & body through professional guidance, yoga and meditation practices.

How much weight can I lose at Kairali - The Ayurvedic Healing Village Retreat?

Kairali - The Ayurvedic Healing Village’s weight loss program is one of the most result-oriented programs. Since weight is usually an indicator of other stress points/issues in our bio-system, the weight loss program varies for every individual depending upon their respective Body Constitutions and the Nature of Obesity. Hence, we don’t guarantee or mention how much weight loss one will achieve; but we can assure you the best results
