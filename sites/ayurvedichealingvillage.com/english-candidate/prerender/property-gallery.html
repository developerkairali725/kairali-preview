Arrival and reception

6 photographs

Patients arrive through the tiled Kerala-style gatehouse and are received in the reception lounge, under a high beamed ceiling with the founders' portraits on the brick walls. The round Kairali emblem and the trophy shelves stand behind the reception desk.

The treatment block and its interiors

14 photographs

Inside the treatment block, arched wooden doors marked Panchakarma open off a skylit atrium with tiered stone fountains. The consultation room, the in-house pharmacy dispensary, the waiting lounge and the corridors are shown as they are used.

Gardens, water and grounds

13 photographs

A stone-lined water channel winds between the cottages and lawns, crossed by white-railed timber footbridges. Coconut palms, flowering ixora and the lotus pond frame the brick and laterite buildings; the outdoor pool sits inside the tree canopy.
