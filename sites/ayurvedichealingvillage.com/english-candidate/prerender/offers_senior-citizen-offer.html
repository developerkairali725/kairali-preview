What We Offer

Signature Wellness Programs at Kairali

Each program is crafted specifically for the unique health needs of seniors and ageing parents, guided by Ayurvedic wisdom and modern medical care.

  • Most Popular

    Kairali’s rejuvenation and detoxification healing program

    A comprehensive programme for healthy ageing that aims to support immunity, energy and stamina — suited to seniors with low energy or muscle weakness, after physician assessment. It combines Panchakarma with modern wellness protocols.

  • For Arthritis & Joint Pain

    Joint rehabilitation and arthritis treatment

    Focused therapies and herbal formulations that precisely target joint pain due to ageing, arthritis, and chronic body pain in old age. Includes specialised Janu Basti, Kati Basti, and herbal steam therapies for lasting relief.

  • For Mind & Mood

    Digestive wellness and gut health yoga therapy

    Calming treatments and mind-body practices that ease worry, restlessness, and emotional fatigue — promoting deep mental peace for both seniors and their caregivers. Includes Shirodhara, meditation, and pranayama sessions.

  • For Digestion & Energy

    Digestive Harmony & Detox

    Gentle detoxification and digestive support to reduce bloating, heaviness, and sluggishness — helping improve energy, clarity, and overall wellbeing for ageing parents. Herbal formulations paired with therapeutic diet plans.
