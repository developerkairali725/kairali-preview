Our approach focuses on the person’s overall wellbeing. Any Ayurvedic supportive care should be discussed with the treating oncologist and must not replace or delay prescribed cancer treatment.

We welcome people at different stages: those recently diagnosed, those currently receiving cancer treatment, and those who have completed chemotherapy or other treatment. A healthy lifestyle and a balanced diet support overall wellbeing, and the calm surroundings of the hospital offer a quiet place to rest during your stay.

At Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village, authentic Ayurvedic massages and therapies are offered to support comfort, relaxation and a positive frame of mind. Therapies such as Abhyangam, Shirodhara and Vaman are chosen according to the patient’s condition after consultation, and yoga sessions are arranged to help with calm and relaxation.
