About Kairali

KAIRALI AYURVEDIC GROUP is headed by joint Managing Directors Mrs. Gita Ramesh and Mr. K.V. Ramesh, both of whom hail from a long family lineage of traditional Ayurvedic doctors. Blessed with a legacy of expertise emanating from such renowned physicians as the late Dr. Sankunny Vaidiar (grandfather of Mr. K. V. Ramesh) and the Dr. K. S. Vasudevan (father of Mr. K. V. Ramesh), Kairali Group was established on a solid ground of Ayurveda research. Since its inception, Kairali has built on the achievements of its forefathers, developing its product infrastructure to make a diverse range of Ayurvedic products available to a multi-national audience.

TREATMENT CENTRES Keen to promote the healing effects of an Ayurvedic lifestyle, Kairali established our first Treatment Centre in New Delhi, 1989. The success of the treatments and therapies provided there led to the proliferation of demand for Kairali facilities. Our Treatment Centres can now be found throughout India, and we remain an industry favourite for education and staff training. Our regular workshops and training courses attract the participation of doctors, dieticians and therapists from all over the world.

HOSPITALITY & HEALTHCARE With an excellent infrastructure firmly in place, Kairali opened The Ayurvedic Healing Village in Palakkad, Kerala, in 1999. Set in a tropical forest landscape and surrounded by organic herbal farmland, the award-winning Healing Village is today an NABH-accredited Ayurvedic hospital where patients come for physician-guided Panchakarma, detoxification and yoga programmes. Kairali’s international profile has since grown significantly: the group also operates a separate hospitality property, Villa Raag in Agonda, Goa, and Ayurvedic wellness centres across three continents.

AYURVEDIC MEDICINE Kairali’s Ayurvedic Herbal Remedies have an impressive legacy in the family’s heritage. Established in 1948 by Dr. K. S. Vasudevan, father of Mr. K.V. Ramesh, Spalabs has been responsible for the creation of several best-selling formulas and health supplements. Based on authentic Ayurvedic ingredients and the traditional processes of our ancient scripture, these original recipes have undergone continuous improvement over the course of the last sixty years, resulting in our modern range of Ayurvedic Herbal Remedies. Kairali’s wide range of Ayurvedic products includes a hundred authentic herbal medicines, skin care, body products and spa equipment, available to our partners and spa franchisees for distribution at their outlets.
