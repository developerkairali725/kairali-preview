The admission

One doctor. One plan. Written for you.

Panchakarma is not a menu of treatments. Your doctor assesses you before arrival, prescribes the therapies, diet and rest, and reviews your progress daily until discharge.

I

Before you arrive

A free video consultation with your doctor. Share your medical records; we confirm whether admission is right for you and how long it should be.

II

During admission

Therapies through the morning, a prescribed diet from the hospital kitchen, gentle yoga and long, unhurried rest. Your doctor reviews your progress throughout your admission.

III

After discharge

A written home regimen and a follow-up consultation, so the routine travels home with you.
