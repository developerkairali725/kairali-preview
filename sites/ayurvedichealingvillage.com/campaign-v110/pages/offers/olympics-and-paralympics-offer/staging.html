Exclusive Offer for Olympic and Paralympic Athletes — Special Rates

Kairali – The Ayurvedic Healing Village is delighted to extend special rates on our wellness packages in recognition of the commitment and accomplishments of Olympic and Paralympic competitors. With the aid of Ayurveda’s healing properties, this unique program aims to assist athletes in resting and recuperating amid Kerala’s serene surroundings.

Our carefully selected therapies are intended to help athletes recuperate from the demanding physical and mental demands they face, regain equilibrium, and increase their general vitality. Abhyangam, Pizhichil, and Kizhi are examples of specialized Ayurvedic treatments designed to reduce muscle tiredness, increase flexibility, and revitalize the body. To improve mental clarity and cultivate inner calm, these therapies are coupled with yoga and meditation practices.

The package also includes personalized consultations with Ayurvedic professionals to design health regimens that address specific requirements, such as healing, detoxification, or improved performance. Visitors can enjoy exquisite lodgings and healthy, wholesome meals designed to promote optimal health.

This exclusive offer highlights the retreat’s dedication to supporting athletes who inspire others through their perseverance and brilliance. Reconnect with yourself and recharge for future challenges in a peaceful, soothing environment that honors your accomplishments and promotes holistic wellness.

Offer eligibility and terms

  • 10% for 3–13 nights, 15% for 14–20 nights and 20% for 21 nights or more.
  • Stays of 1–2 nights are charged at standard rates; offer savings start at 3 nights.
  • The discount applies to the treatment-package price before tax. Separately charged extras are not discounted, and anything included in your package stays included.
  • One offer applies per booking: if more than one offer fits your stay, we apply the one that gives you the best saving, and discounts are not added together.
  • Before you pay, your written quote shows the eligible package amount, your saving, any extras, taxes and the final total.
  • Availability and the change and cancellation terms are stated in your quote before you pay. Once confirmed, the agreed offer is honoured for that booking.
