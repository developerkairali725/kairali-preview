What Should You Discuss Before Planning Senior Care?

For Seniors (60+)

What would you like help understanding?

  • ◆Joint pain or stiffness that makes walking, standing or using stairs difficult.
  • ◆Back pain, neck stiffness or other discomfort in your daily routine.
  • ◆Changes in strength or balance that you want to discuss with the physician.
  • ◆Questions about sleep, digestion, energy or your existing medical care.

Describe your concerns in your own words. An individual consultation guides the appropriate programme and care plan.

For Adult Children & Caregivers

What needs to be clear before you book?

  • ◆Your parent’s health history, current care and questions for the Ayurvedic physician.
  • ◆The residential arrangements and any specific assistance your parent may need.
  • ◆The programme’s minimum stay, your available dates and accompanying-person arrangements.
  • ◆The senior patient’s eligibility and a written quote with taxes and separately charged extras.

Family members can enquire for the patient. The senior-care rate belongs to the eligible senior patient; it does not automatically apply to the person making the enquiry.

Discuss Care for Yourself or Your Parent

Category eligibility and programme rates

This care commitment is for eligible senior patients. Family members may enquire on their behalf; the category benefit applies to the eligible patient who claims it.

  • Only eligible patients who claim the category benefit receive the preferential rate; other patients pay the regular programme rate. Eligible programme rates are 10% for 3–13 nights, 15% for 14–20 nights and 20% for 21 nights or more.
  • Stays of 1–2 nights use regular programme rates. Each residential programme has its own minimum stay; confirm the minimum for your chosen programme.
  • The benefit applies to the treatment-package price before tax. Separately charged extras are excluded, and items included in your package remain included.
  • Benefits do not stack. If more than one benefit applies, the best eligible single benefit is used for the booking.
  • Before payment, your written quote shows the eligible package amount, category benefit, separately charged extras, taxes and final total.
  • Availability and change and cancellation terms are stated in your quote before payment. Once confirmed, the agreed benefit is honoured for that booking.
