## Where AY science and the compliance law CONFLICT — flagged, not guessed

1. **Secret-desire amplitude vs DMR.** The weight-loss MoA's secret desire is a number on a
   scale; the classical MCB would promise it ("lose X without dieting"). DMR/Google Healthcare
   BANS that claim class. Resolution shipped: the MCB pivots to the deeper frustration ("tried
   everything alone") and promises the METHOD, not the outcome — "a programme, not a promise"
   is itself the hook. Flag: a marketer may judge this weaker than a numbers hook; the law
   leaves no alternative, and the honesty IS the differentiation in this category. OWNER CALL
   if he wants the tension resolved differently.
2. **Curiosity vs message match.** MCBs manufacture curiosity; paid-search LPs must CONFIRM
   the query (message match) rather than tease. Resolution: H1 = query phrasing (match) +
   the curiosity/differentiator in the second clause ("— prescribed, not picked"). Flagged as
   the standing pattern for this batch.
3. **Ethical-bribe MCB (free content net) vs 1:1 attention ratio.** A downloadable
   free-content offer would add a second conversion goal to a single-CTA page. Not shipped
   tonight; belongs to the KIT/remarketing lane, not paid LPs. Flagged for the N1–N3 builds.
