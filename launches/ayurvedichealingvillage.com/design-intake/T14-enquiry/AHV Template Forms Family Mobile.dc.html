What we do with your enquiry

Every enquiry arrives at the front office in Palakkad and is logged the same day. The patient care team separates administrative questions — admission dates, travel, accommodation, documents — from clinical ones. Clinical questions go to an Ayurvedic doctor before anyone replies to you.

You do not need a diagnosis to write to us. A description in your own words is enough. If you already have reports, blood work or a discharge summary, say so in the message and the patient care team will tell you where to send them.

What to include

  • The concern in your own words, and how long it has been present.
  • Any current medication, and any condition under treatment elsewhere.
  • The dates you could travel, even approximately.
  • Who the programme is for, if it is not for you.

Admission, in order

  1. Enquiry. You send this form. The patient care team acknowledges it.
  2. Consultation. An Ayurvedic doctor speaks with you, by video or telephone.
  3. Programme proposal. A written plan with duration, treatments and cost.
  4. Admission. Dates confirmed, prescription drawn up on arrival after examination.
