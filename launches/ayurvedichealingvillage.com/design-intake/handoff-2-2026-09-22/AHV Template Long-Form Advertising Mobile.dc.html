Ayurvedic Treatments - Your Answer to all Health Issues:

The human body is made up of three components as per Ayurveda - Vata, Pitta, and Kapha.

Space and air make up the vata dosha, which controls all mental and physical movement. It regulates breathing, the flow of blood, the removal of waste, and mental cognitive processes. Fire and water, which make up Pitta dosha, control all heat, metabolism, and mental and physical metamorphosis. It regulates how we process our sensory inputs and how we break down food. We can distinguish between right and wrong because of it. Finally, all structure and lubrication in the body and mind are governed by kapha, which is composed of earth and water. It regulates the weight, development, and lubrication of the lungs and joints as well as the production of every bodily tissue.
