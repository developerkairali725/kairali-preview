**Status: PREPARED, not shipped. One read from you and it goes live together with the 301s.**

Owner ruling 2026-09-21: *"Merge all content into one and 301 the other two, whichever has better
older created date or whatever."*

## What the three actually are, measured

| `/privacy-policy/` | — | 2025-02-06 | 867 | 30 with new-privacy-policy |

| `/privacy-statement/` | 2025-02-24 | 2026-05-13 | 1,468 | **zero with either** |

Two facts decide the merge:

1. **`/new-privacy-policy/` is very nearly a superset of `/privacy-policy/`** — 30 of the latter's
   38 long sentences appear in it verbatim, plus 21 more. Despite the name, it is also the OLDEST
   by creation date and the MOST RECENTLY modified.
2. **`/privacy-statement/` is a completely independent document** — zero shared sentences with
   either. It is also the only one that names a law (**DPDP, GDPR, CCPA**) or a retention period
   (**7, 3 and 8 years**). Legally it is the serious one; the other two name no law, no retention
   period and no data-subject rights.

So there is nothing to reconcile: the documents do not contradict, they cover different ground.

**URL: `/privacy-policy/`** — the conventional address, the one the footer links, and the one any
external site or browser bookmark would point at. Not the oldest-created URL, because
`/new-privacy-policy/` is an unshippable name for a public legal page.

## Three things in the source text you should see before it ships

1. **The effective date is "1 st April, 2017"** — nine years old, on the document that would become
   the site's only privacy policy. `/privacy-statement/` separately says "Last Update: February 18,
   2025". The merged page cannot honestly carry both.
2. **The contact block is a NEW DELHI address** — *D-130, Ambedkar Colony, Mehrauli, New Delhi -
   110030* — not the Palakkad hospital. That is presumably the registered office, but on a privacy
   policy it is the address a data subject writes to, so it should be the right one deliberately.
3. **The contact email is Cloudflare-obfuscated in the source** and decodes to a typo'd address
   elsewhere on the live site (see Q-AHV-CONTACT-ADDRESSES). The merged page should carry
   `info@kairali.com` plainly.

## Why this is prepared rather than shipped

This is legal text governing patient data on a NABH-accredited hospital's site, and the merged
document would cite GDPR, DPDP and CCPA with specific retention periods. I can assemble it
mechanically — and everything above is assembled, not authored, with the source of every section
named — but a merged privacy policy should be read by a person before it governs anything. That is
one read, not a project.

**On your word the merge ships and these two redirects go into the map at the same time:**

```
/privacy-statement/    301 -> /privacy-policy/
/new-privacy-policy/   301 -> /privacy-policy/
```

They land together deliberately. Redirecting first would point visitors at the 867-word document
and LOSE the only GDPR/DPDP-compliant text on the site — strictly worse than today.
