## D-DESIGN-DIFF similarity verdict

**Genuinely distinct as a composition — not recolor-mistakable — but the mid-page connective tissue is arogyashala.in re-skinned.** The identity carriers are real firsts for the fleet: full-bleed photo hero with in-photo wordmark (no sibling opens on a photo), oversized Sanskrit programme editorial with bronze English overlines, the honest year-round admissions strip, and the leaf-spined day timeline — no other one-pager has any of them, and vs healing.recipes (book shelf) or arogyakendra.com (split ivory hero, trust cards, sticky CTA) the page cannot be confused. However, below the hero the build reuses arogyashala.in's device set nearly verbatim — doors (incl. data-cta names), the +/– FAQ with an identical section-heading pair, the giant-numeral ordered list, the hero-overlap card trick — three **new** repeating device families beyond the two known repeats. Known repeat #1 (WhatsApp + email pill pair) is present in the ladder/footer; known repeat #2 (locked-facts dl / trust band) is largely avoided — heritage facts moved to footer fine print, and the What-to-expect dl is a different, content-native use. Net: distinct at first glance, familial at second scroll; fix the verbatim FAQ heading first (finding 7).
