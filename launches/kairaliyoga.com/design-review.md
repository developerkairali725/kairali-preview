## Detailed findings

1. **Kurale misrenders the Sanskrit names — the signature device ships typographically wrong.**
   The entire concept is "Sanskrit-forward oversized-Kurale editorial", and in every display instance Kurale paints the IAST macron over the *following consonant*, not the vowel: "Dhyan̄a-Yoga Shibiram", "Dip̄anam" (dotless ı, bar over p), "Pran̄a", "Sam̄ya" (critic-sanskrit-dhyana/agni/prana.png, DPR 3). The DOM is correct (curl-verified U+0101/U+012B precomposed); Asap renders the identical string correctly in the door hint on the same page. Probes committed: NFC vs NFD normalization changes nothing (critic-macron-probe-nfc-nfd-asap.png), and **Google-CDN Kurale reproduces the defect exactly** (critic-macron-probe-cdn-kurale.png) — this is a mark-anchoring defect in the Kurale font itself, not in next/font subsetting. To the exact audience this page targets — yoga practitioners and TTC seekers who read IAST — the site misspells its own three products at 59px. The manual (ch.04) mandates Kurale for display, so the resolution needs a ruling, not a hack (fix 1).
2. **Image discipline is missing — 3.6MB to a phone-first audience.**
   Measured first load at 375×812: 3,643KB uncompressed total; images 3,066KB of it. The eager hero is 733KB (1600×1067, `fetchPriority=high`, no srcset — the same file serves a 375px phone and a 2560-device-px desktop, where it is *upscaled* at DPR 2); the expect-figure grounds photo is 671KB rendered ~380px wide; farm 389KB, food 346KB, dhara 327KB — and Chromium's lazy-load margins fetch all eight photos at/near load (request log committed in the audit JSON). `sips -Z 1600 quality 82` (IMAGE_MANIFEST) was a copy step, not an optimization pass. The hero photo carries the grade for this audience — it must also carry a byte budget.
3. **The day-timeline's midday stop shows food where it says therapy.**
   "Midday · One therapy, every day — …herbal steam baths included…" is illustrated by idiyappam and vegetable stew (critic-timeline-midday-desktop.png/-mobile.png), while the catalogue's one therapy-signifier image — the therapist carrying the dhara pot at sunrise — illustrates "Morning · The physician's check-in" (a therapist is not the physician, either). Meals are spoken of at the Evening stop, which shows meditation. The time-axis device the research demanded is realized in structure and photography quality, but its image column tells a slightly different day than its text column — on a device whose whole point is pre-living the day accurately.
4. **Desktop composition goes single-lung for four sections.**
   At 1280, programmes, training, FAQ and the enquiry ladder all live in the left 720px (right edges measured at x=820) with ~360px of empty ivory to the right — the same "restrained-verging-on-unfinished" pattern the arogyashala review docked (its finding 6), now recurring on the fleet's showcase E-register build, including on the signature programme editorial. The zigzag timeline, expect, teachers and voices sections prove the page knows how to use the width; the four prose-measure sections never answer it. Composition polish, not a gate item — but it is now a *fleet pattern*, and the E register is the one place it should not appear.
5. **The proof block is a dignitary guestbook, not this buyer's proof.**
   The research device was "named-person testimonial + review-count proof block" (Sampoorna/Power of Now). What ships is three legacy VIP quotes — a Government of India Joint Secretary (title ~two decades stale), "**Late** Pramod Mahajan (former Cabinet Minister…)", and "Mrs. & Mr. Ajitabh Bachhan (London)" — none of whom mentions yoga, in guestbook register ("Paradise on Earth…"), one of them flagged deceased inside a testimonial card (critic-voices-desktop.png). Rule 6 makes this the honest maximum today (they are the only verified voices, correctly provenance-noted "As published on the previous kairaliyoga.com"), and the *device chrome* is right — but for a US/DE retreat comparison shopper this section reads dated-institutional rather than peer-proof. Framing is fixable now; real proof (counted ratings, yoga-traveller quotes) needs collection — flag to Abilash.
6. **Micro findings.** (a) Footer "Terms" link 38.1px wide (its row is 44px tall; the flow-link width law is soft, but the fix is one padding line). (b) The hero source at 1600w is upscaled on DPR-2 desktops ≥1280 — the money shot is very slightly soft on retina; a 2560w rung in the srcset fix (fix 2) covers it. (c) door_restore hint/anchor mismatch (promises two programmes, lands on one, and Prāna Sāmya is a full scroll below with nothing binding the pair). (d) Two half-step interpolations beyond the printed source: "one-to-one" (FAQ 1) and "given by the hospital's own therapists" (timeline) — align to the PDFs' words or verify with Abilash.
