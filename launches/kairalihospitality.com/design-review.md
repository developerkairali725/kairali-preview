## Detailed findings

1. **The consent banner cuts the first door's title in half at mobile first paint — the page's hinge, damaged at its one unrepeatable moment.**
   Measured at 375×812, no stored consent: banner fixed at bottom, 163.3px tall, top y=648.8; hero statement (h1 bottom 530.7) and place strip (bottom 573.1) fully clear; door 1 kicker "STAY" clear; but door 1's title spans y 637.2→661.1 — **bisected mid-glyph, 11.6px of 23.9px visible** — and its hint ("The three places ↓") is entirely under the banner (critic-mobile-doorclip-zoom.png shows the sliced line "I want to stay at a Kairali property"). The site's own CSS states the law it breaks: "the hero statement AND the first door must read above the consent banner" (globals.css @media block). After a consent choice the composition is excellent (critic-mobile-firstview-after-consent.png) — but a first visit only gets one first paint. Arithmetic says the fix is one number: hero 76svh→70svh recovers ~49px; title needs 12.3px, title+hint ~50px.
2. **The Villa Raag panel crops its subject out.**
   The 3:4 portrait crop of the landscape original (1200×799, default `object-position: 50% 50%`) shows the covered terrace's dark underside and a hedge; the plunge pool with the floral mosaic — the stated subject of the photograph and the panel's alt text — survives only as a dark corner sliver (critic-panels-1440.png, left panel; same at 375). The panel that must sell the group's boutique property to a premium traveller currently reads "roof and shrubs." One line of per-panel `object-position` (or a portrait-friendlier frame from the raag library) recovers it.
3. **The Healing Village panel is half text, and the panel row loses its calm.**
   The Singita/Oetker device the research commissioned is "location overline + property name + one line max — the image does the selling." Villa Raag's copy block occupies ~38% of the panel; the Healing Village's three-line description plus two stacked CTA rows occupy ~60%, pushing its overline to ~33% from the panel top while its neighbours sit at ~62%/56% — the three text blocks land at three different heights and the eye zigzags across the row (critic-panels-1440.png). The clinical register does not require this much copy: one clause carries NABH + physician-led; the rest is doing veil-duty over a photograph that is the row's best crop.
4. **The Czech panel reads as a missing image on mobile.**
   At 375 the forced 3:4 ratio yields a 335×447 forest-700 block with ~55% empty field above the copy, stacked directly under two photographic panels (critic-czech-panel-mobile.png). On desktop the emptiness is compositional (finding table above); in a single-column stack it looks like a broken loader. The honest no-photo ruling (IMAGE_MANIFEST GAP) does not force the ratio on mobile — let the dark panel hug its content below 760px and keep the full-height panel in the desktop row.
5. **Tablet band (760–979px): the panel stack pins left.**
   `.panel { max-width: 560px }` with no centering: measured at 768, all three panels sit at x=20 with a 188px dead right gutter (critic-tablet-768-panels.png). One `margin-inline: auto` finishes the viewport the method walks between phone and desktop.
6. **The page ships 2.2MB to a phone, with no responsive images.**
   Measured on the 375 walkthrough: 2,204KB total — images 1,649KB (hero-villa-raag-dusk 437KB = the LCP, panel-healing-village 456KB, panel-villa-raag-pool 300KB, develop pair 446KB), script 345KB, fonts 147KB. Every image is a bare `<img>` with fixed src: the phone downloads the same 1600px hero and 1200px panels as a 5K desktop. Panels are lazy-loaded (right call) but all load on the walk. This rides the Lighthouse ≥90 machine gate rather than this one, but a "premium traveller on a phone" is the named audience — srcset/sizes (or next/image) is the fix, and the hero can lose ~40% at q70 without visible cost.
7. **The "two-line statement" is a three-line statement.**
   At 375, 1280 and 1440 the h1 renders three lines — sentence 2 wraps at max-width 24ch (all hero screenshots). It happens to break cleanly ("A family practising / Ayurveda for 118 years.") and reads well, but the commissioned device (§5.1/§7, Singita two-line pattern) never appears as specified. Either shorten sentence 2 so the form is real, or bless the three-line setting in the build brief — don't leave the research and the page disagreeing.

This is the strongest concept execution the factory has produced: every commissioned device exists, the opening is genuinely cinematic, the facts layer is airtight (computed years, no JV ownership claim anywhere a machine or human reads, locked contacts, clinical lexicon verified zero-hit on the rendered DOM), the analytics are the most attributable yet, and the token audit is the cleanest of the four reviewed sites — the focus-ring and share-card mistakes of the earlier launches were pre-empted, not repeated. Fixes 1–5 are CSS-or-one-line cheap; fix 6 is an afternoon. A focused pass should clear 9.0+ on iteration 2 of 3 — the scorecard goes to the builder unsoftened.
