## Why ONE direction shared across both domains

The research argues SHARED (niche brief §6): sister desks of one manufacturer, deliberately cross-linked — a buyer who follows "want this under your own brand?" must land somewhere that is recognisably the same company. One system also halves build and approval cost. Distinction lives in the **hero treatment and accent temperature**, not in a second design system: **oils = warm/material** (the liquid itself — golden oil, brass, wood; marigold carries the "liquid gold" note) · **products = cool/process** (the facility — deep forest surfaces, leaf accent, laboratory and batch imagery).

## The direction: "The Batch Ledger"

A pharma batch record made beautiful. The niche's mid-tier decorates with badges; KAPPL's difference is that its credibility is REAL — so the design's job is to present artefacts and specifications with the composure of a printed ledger, and let restraint read as confidence. Memorable ≠ loud here: in a SERP full of cluttered exporter sites, the sexy move is the calm one ("sexy sells" bar met by contrast with the niche, not by noise).

1. **Hero = the ledger plate (the signature element).** Split composition: left, an oversized Kurale display statement (weight 400, never bold) over ivory; right, a raised **specification card** — a Lato-set table carrying only verified facts (pack formats 5000 ml / 1000 g, cert strip as published, dispatch scope) with the RFQ CTA seated inside the card. The spec card IS the hero image frame: B2B buyers scan for specs before they scroll [niche brief §3/S19], so the conversion surface and the aesthetic signature are the same object.
2. **Certificates as plaques, not icons.** The published GMP certificate artefacts (kappl.co.in) rendered as framed plaques in a `/quality` wall and a compressed strip on every page. Never an invented badge row.
3. **Oversized Lato numerals** for the published counters (10000+ · 1200+ · 900+ · 111+) and pack sizes ("5 L" set huge). Numbers are this niche's jewellery — but only owner-published numbers, ever.
4. **Colour law (existing tokens ONLY — no new tokens, no new ramp steps; polish time-box respected):** ivory `--ivory` page ground · forest `--forest-500` interactive/headers · `--forest-700/800` dark bands (hero underlay, footer) · bronze `--bronze-400` overlines / `--bronze-600` body-bronze on light · marigold `--marigold-400` accent only, `--marigold-300` accent-on-dark. Error/info semantics untouched.
5. **Type law:** Kurale h1–h3 (400) · Asap body/UI · Lato captions, overlines, numerals, spec tables. Botanical names italic (ch.11) — *Bala*, *Guluchi*, *Triphala* italicised in formulation copy.
6. **Imagery:** real only — mined store/facility photography (manifest in docs/media/) at build; where gaps exist, generation prompts are DRAFTED for human-run tools per the asset roster (ambient/product only; hero brief: "top-down brass uruli with slow-pouring golden oil on dark wood, natural window light, imperfect droplets"). No AI-generic wellness stock, no fake people.
7. **Doctrine compliance built in:** single primary CTA per surface (RFQ) · no carousels, no desktop hamburger, no scrolljacking · floating stack ≤3 (consent + interim Chatbase bubble + sticky WhatsApp bar, single-slot law) · thumb-zone mobile CTAs · poster-first LCP, no autoplay.
