## 1 · MoA (Step 6) — who this page talks to

MoA = the procurement buyer at a spa/hotel, panchakarma centre/clinic, or a
distributor/importer (BRIEF §Who it is for; niche brief §3 NDFF signals).
NDFF read: **Need** — reliable therapy-volume restock with documentation;
**Desire** — one certified manufacturer-direct line, no surprises; **Fear** —
adulterated/repacked stock, unverifiable claims, being burned by a trader;
**Frustration** — opaque quoting, fake precision ("flexible MOQs" that mean
nothing). **Secret desire** — running a supply line so dependable it makes
their own operation look best-in-class.

## 2 · MCB check (Step 1 — "How to [secret desire] without [pain]")

  "Manufacturer-direct bulk supply … **No trading layer, no repacking: every
  batch is quoted direct from our Pollachi facility.**" → maps to *how to keep
  classical oils in stock at therapy grade* **without** *traders, repacking and
  provenance doubt*. PASS.

  card; header demoted to ghost). PASS — curiosity/hand-raise, no pressure.

## 5 · LOOK check (owner addendum: psychologically attractive to the MoA? ROI lens)

Judged on launches/bulkayurvedaoils.com/screens/ (desktop 1280 + mobile 375, viewport + full):

- **Visual hierarchy:** first paint = statement → spec card → one green CTA.
  For a procurement scanner the spec card IS the hero — specs before scroll,
  which is exactly this MoA's reading order. The 5 L / 1 kg numerals act as
  scan beacons. STRONG.
- **Trust signals per awareness stage:** cert strip + as-published qualifier in
  the first viewport (card) and repeated in the dark strip; heritage line under
  it; real bottle photography (recognisably the actual product a buyer would
  restock — an authenticity cue no stock photo gives). STRONG.
- **CTA psychology:** one solid primary repeated at decision points (card,
  lanes, sticky mobile bar), always the same words — "Get a bulk quote" names
  the exact next action, no friction words. Mobile keeps thumb-zone access via
  the sticky bar. STRONG.
- **ROI read:** the shortest path from landing → hand-raise is one viewport on
  desktop and two thumb-lengths on mobile; every section funnels to #rfq. The
  page optimises for RFQ leads/mo (the register KPI), not for admiration.
- **Weaknesses (honest):** (a) the hero photo is a tight crop of a landscape
  original — serviceable, but a purpose-shot vertical (brass-uruli pour, per
  the asset roster) would raise the desire emotion a step; (b) no Phase-3
  proof faces/names (see §3 residual). Neither blocks; both are named asset/
  owner asks.

## 6 · Changes made because of this check

1. Hero lede sharpened to the explicit MCB "without" clause ("No trading layer,
   no repacking …") rather than a generic supply line.

   repacked stock) — the page acknowledges the buyer's reality before selling.
3. Process step 3 carries the honesty register ("confirmed per formulation …
   never guessed here") — the anti-fake-precision objection strip.
