## 1 · The ONE direction: "The Foundry Ledger"

A **heritage manufacturer's ledger, set in a dark workshop.** Where the fleet's clinical and hospitality surfaces are ivory-first and airy, the equipment surface opens on **forest-700 dark ground** (ch.08 dark-surface token) — the workshop at dusk, brass glinting — with the catalog card sitting on it like a lit specimen. The emotional register is *craftsmanship and capability*: a buyer equipping a hospital or resort should feel they've walked into the factory of the only maker that also runs its own healing village. Not consumer-wellness, not AI-template SaaS. The signature UI is the **spec ledger** — Lato-numeral spec tables and capability counters ruled with hairlines, everywhere: hero, category cards, PDPs. B2B buyers scan specs; we make the spec table the aesthetic.

**Why this is not boring (the sexy-sells bar):** every direct competitor is a white brochure site from 2012; the global premium tier (Gharieni) is dark-accented and confident. A dark, brass-lit, serif-display manufacturer site is instantly memorable in this SERP — and it is built entirely from the existing brand palette, so it stays unmistakably Kairali.
