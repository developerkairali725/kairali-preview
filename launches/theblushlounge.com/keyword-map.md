## Cannibalization and truth rules

- Home owns the broad artist/entity query; Services owns service-detail intent; Contact owns booking/contact intent.
- The Journal owns educational intent. The colour-correction guide does not compete with Services for booking intent.
- No page targets opening-hours, price-list or exact-location queries until those facts are confirmed.
- No “best”, ranking, guaranteed-outcome or unsupported award claim is used.
- English is the only declared locale. No hreflang cluster is required.
