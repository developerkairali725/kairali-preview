## What this site is

The brand home of **Kairali Organics** — the group's **100% chemical-free** Ayurvedic product line (D3). An **informational brand shell**: product-line presentation + enquiry. **NO cart, no checkout, no prices** — the buy-path stays Track-B-gated; purchase intent is handed off to kairaliproducts.in/.com and the marketplaces (BigBasket · Flipkart · Amazon), each hand-off a tracked event.

This is a **REBUILD of a live legacy site** (ASPX brochure site, mined 18 Jul — inventory in the mining doc §2), not a greenfield: Build Decision Matrix applies, protection map is in the STRATEGY artifact, and the legacy site stays live until Abilash flips DNS (human action).

## The brand boundary (the one rule that can sink this pair)

- Kairali Organics = the NINE proprietary products the live site presents (Kairbossom · Kairwash · Kairpack · Kaircin · Kairoil · Kairbal · Kairheal · Kairkare Plus · Kairkare), positioned as 100% chemical-free Ayurvedic formulations.
- **KAIR is a DIFFERENT brand** (soaps/shampoos/moisturizing cream/body wash — NOT fully chemical-free) and lives at kairbykairali.com (its live, canonical home per D-KAIR-CANONICAL, 18 Jul; kairproducts.com 301s into it). This site never lists KAIR products; KAIR never inherits "chemical-free". One labelled sister-ranges block is the only crossing.
- **The naming trap** (mining doc §6): the nine Organics products all carry the Kair- prefix while the KAIR brand's cosmetics mostly do not. Both sites state their own brand definition explicitly in the hero and About copy.

## Who it is for

1. **The Ayurveda-modern buyer** (comp-set §5: 30–50, trusts traditional ingredients, wants modern presentation) researching the brand before buying on a marketplace.
2. **Brand-navigational searchers** — people who typed "kairali organic" or a product name (kaircin, kairoil…) and must land on an owned, current, on-message surface instead of today's fragmented SERP (comp-set §3).
3. **Enquirers** — distributors, bulk/gifting, and product questions → info@kairali.com.

## What it must achieve

1. **Own the brand SERP** for "kairali organic(s)" and the nine product brand terms (Organization schema, sameAs, sitelink-ready IA — comp-set §3).
2. **Present the line credibly** at the Forest-Essentials/Kama design bar ("sexy sells") using the 583-image library's product + range photography.
3. **Route demand**: outbound store/marketplace clicks + enquiry submissions, all instrumented (data-cta).
4. **Carry only owned claims**: chemical-free (D3 + live-site copy) · dosha-compliance philosophy (mined) · WHO-GMP manufacturing (KAPPL) · heritage strictly per the lineage doc's approved lines. **Never "certified organic"** (no cert exists in the estate). No efficacy/disease claims.
