## 3 · The LOOK — is the rendered page psychologically attractive to this MoA? (owner addendum; ROI lens)

Judged against the committed screenshots (`launches/kairaliorganic.com/screens/2026-07-18-golden-{desktop-1440x900,desktop-full,mobile-390x844,mobile-full}.png`):

- **Visual hierarchy:** one eye-path — claim overline → serif promise → dosha line → proof link → single marigold CTA (the page's ONE accent moment, so the eye lands on the conversion action). No competing accents anywhere else above the fold. PASS.
- **Imagery:** the real range photography is the trust argument itself — ivory apothecary cartons with botanical engravings echo the site's own ledger identity; nothing stocky, nothing AI. For a 30–50 Ayurveda-modern buyer this reads "heritage house, modern hands" — the Forest-Essentials/Kama bar the brief set. PASS.
- **CTA psychology:** "Meet the Nine" = curiosity (right emotion for a cold/navigational visitor), "Where to buy" = intent capture for warm visitors, "What chemical-free means here ↓" = the skeptic's proof path — three temperatures, one per awareness stage, none pressuring. Store CTAs reappear at the Phase-4 position with high contrast. PASS.
- **Trust signals per awareness stage:** provenance lockup above the fold (unaware) → no-certification honesty + ingredient transparency (problem/solution-aware) → WHO-GMP + 1948/1989/1908 lineage counters (product-aware) → named-not-linked marketplaces (most-aware, honest). PASS.
- **ROI:** the shell's funnel metric is outbound store clicks + enquiries; every such control carries a `data-cta` event and ≥44px touch target; mobile ledger is a horizontal scroller with 75px rows. The chat bubble renders (facade-loaded) without competing with the hero CTA.
