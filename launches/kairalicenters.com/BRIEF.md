## What this site is

The Kairali Ayurvedic Group's **treatment-center network site** — the digital front door for the group's **14 centers across five countries** (counts always rendered from `@kairali/facts`, never typed). Register A (group/network): non-revenue **lead generation** — the conversion is a qualified enquiry (WhatsApp, call, form, consultation booking) routed to the right center. Organic site class in the gate registry. Canonical spelling: **kairaliCENTERS** in domain/GSC/copy (D-KC-DOMAIN); "centre" survives only inside registered proper names.

This is a FROM-SCRATCH rebuild, not an enrichment of the current 19-route build. The current build's verified strengths carry forward as inputs (facts-module roster import, floating-stack de-confliction, data-cta vocabulary, honest-gap pattern); its pages do not.

## Who it is for

1. **Local patients** in the 8 Indian center cities (Delhi ×2, Gurgaon, Faridabad, Guwahati, Raipur, Patna, Vadodara) searching "ayurvedic centre / panchakarma in <city>" — the primary revenue audience.
2. **International clients** near the 6 franchise-partner centers (Japan ×3, Mauritius, Czech Republic, Kuwait).
3. **Remote patients anywhere** — the online-consultation funnel (the answer for every city where we have no center).
4. **Corporate wellness buyers** (legacy content proves the intent exists).
5. **Franchise prospects** (secondary; presentation here, canonical franchise funnel on ayurvedafranchise.com per Decision #4).

## What it must achieve

1. **Absorb 100% of the legacy site's ranking value** before any DNS flip — every one of the ~110 legacy indexable URLs has an explicit KEEP/MERGE/301 fate (protection map, mining audit §4); "lean at birth, complete at launch."
2. **Rank per-city**: one uniquely-written, schema-complete page per center (`/centers/<slug>`), brand+location titles, no doorway templating; `/regions/delhi-ncr` takes the metro query where we have 4 centers.
3. **Convert**: every center page carries call / WhatsApp / email / directions affordances plus a consent-bearing enquiry form routed to **info@kairali.com** with a hidden center field.
4. **Tell the network truth**: all six partner-branded centers presented as **franchise partners** (D-KC-PARTNERS) — which doubles as the /franchise recruitment proof; NAP gaps (Raipur hours, Czech domain) stay FLAGGED as "to be confirmed", never invented (Abishek's lane).
5. **Meet the fleet bar**: design gate ≥ 9.0, Lighthouse ≥ 90 perf / 100 SEO, compliance lint zero, front-gate law end to end.
