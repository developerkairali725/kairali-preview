## Why ONE direction shared across both domains

The research argues SHARED (niche brief §6): sister desks of one manufacturer, deliberately cross-linked — a buyer who follows "need ready-made stock instead?" must land somewhere recognisably the same company. ## The direction: "The Batch Ledger"

A pharma batch record made beautiful. The mid-tier competition decorates with badges; KAPPL's credibility is real, so the design presents artefacts and specifications with the composure of a printed ledger — restraint reading as confidence. In a category of cluttered exporter sites, the calm site is the memorable one ("sexy sells" met by contrast with the niche).

1. **Hero = the ledger plate (signature element).** Split composition: left, oversized Kurale display statement (400, never bold); right, a raised **project card** — a Lato-set table of the four published engagement models (Third Party ×2 · Loan Licence ×2) with the intake CTA seated inside the card. The buyer's first decision ("which model am I?") is the hero's interactive object — conversion surface and aesthetic signature are the same thing.
2. **Certificates as plaques, not icons.** Published GMP certificate artefacts rendered as framed plaques (`/quality` wall + compressed strip sitewide). Never an invented badge.
3. **Oversized Lato numerals** for the published counters (10000+ · 1200+ · 900+ · 111+) and the published "30 days" launch figure. Owner-published numbers only.
4. **Colour law (existing tokens ONLY — no new tokens, no new ramp steps; polish time-box respected):** this domain runs the COOL pole — `--forest-700/800` hero underlay and section bands over ivory, `--leaf-500` accents/focus, `--marigold-300` reserved for the single accent moment on dark, bronze overlines. Same system, different temperature to the oils site.
5. **Type law:** Kurale h1–h3 (400) · Asap body/UI · Lato captions, overlines, numerals, model/spec tables. Botanical names italic (ch.11).
6. **Imagery:** real only — kappl.co.in facility/3D/pictures assets mined at build (manifest in docs/media/); gaps get DRAFTED generation prompts for human-run tools per the asset roster (ambient/process only; hero brief: "stainless manufacturing line detail with copper vessel warmth in bokeh, honest factory light"). No AI-generic labs, no fake people.
7. **Doctrine compliance built in:** single primary CTA per surface (Start your project) · no carousels, no desktop hamburger, no scrolljacking · floating stack ≤3 (consent + interim Chatbase + sticky WhatsApp, single-slot law) · thumb-zone mobile CTAs · poster-first LCP.
