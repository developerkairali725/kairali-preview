## 1 · MoA (Step 6) — who this page talks to

MoA = the brand founder / D2C seller launching an Ayurvedic line; the
established brand or exporter adding an India base; the loan-licence seeker
(BRIEF §Who it is for). NDFF read: **Need** — a licensed, certified line and a
compliant path to market; **Desire** — their own branded range without capex;
**Fear** — being one of a contract manufacturer's forgotten small clients;
quality that embarrasses their brand; **Frustration** — "flexible MOQs" teasers
and gated portals that waste weeks. **Secret desire** — *being known as a
brand owner* — their name on the shelf (AY: the MCB must be rooted here).

## 5 · LOOK check (owner addendum: psychologically attractive to the MoA? ROI lens)

Judged on launches/bulkayurvedaproducts.com/screens/ (desktop 1280 + mobile 375, viewport + full):

- **Visual hierarchy:** dark authority plate makes the ivory model card the
  brightest object on screen — the buyer's first decision ("which model am I?")
  is literally the visual focus. Chevron affordances make the rows read as
  doors, not decoration (the pass-2 fix, verified in pixels). STRONG.
- **Trust signals per awareness stage:** cert band lands inside the first
  viewport (verified in the desktop screenshot); counters with provenance line
  immediately after; facility story one scroll deeper — trust is sequenced from
  claim → number → substance, matching a solution-aware buyer. STRONG.
- **CTA psychology:** "Start your project" is ownership language (the MoA's
  secret desire verbatim — *their* project), repeated identically at card,
  tiles and sticky bar; single solid primary per view. STRONG.
- **ROI read:** the model card converts the highest-intent visitors in
  viewport 1; everything else exists to answer objections and return the reader
  to #start. Optimised for manufacturing enquiries/mo (the register KPI).
- **Weaknesses (honest):** (a) the hero photography is the classical product
  range, honestly captioned — a REAL facility/line photograph (the auditors'
  strongest recommendation) does not exist in the image library yet, and a
  founder-MoA buys capacity proof with their eyes; this is the page's biggest
  psychological gap and is flagged as an asset-mining ask, not faked; (b) no
  named brand references (see §3). Neither blocks; both are named asks.
