## W1 — Voice of Guest Review Mining

- STEP 1 source verification: TripAdvisor, Google Reviews, HolidayCheck.de (DACH properties/markets), Booking.com (Kerala/India), MakeMyTrip (India domestic), Otzovik.com/VK (CIS). Confirm real accessible URLs before mining.
- STEP 2 time window: Tier 1A = last 90 days; Tier 1B = last 180 days; if <15 reviews, expand to 6 months and note.
- STEP 3 extraction: one row per distinct claim; Verbatim Quote = exact guest words, never paraphrase; Operational Claim Made = factual restatement, no editorialising; NEVER fill "Kairali Current State" (HOD-only column); minimum 15 rows per session or document the gap.
