Nakshatra Villa Programme — Spec & Playbook,
Each of our 30 villas is named after a nakshatra (a star of the Indian sky). This workbook turns that into a room-by-room scheme your team can build and track.,
HOW TO USE THIS WORKBOOK,
Start Here,"This tab — the why, the design rules, the sources. For understanding & training."
Action Sheet,The room-by-room checklist to DELEGATE and TRACK. Assign an Owner and a Status per room. This is the doing tab.
Procurement,"The consolidated buy / plant list, so sourcing can be ordered in one go."
Welcome Cards,The in-room welcome-card text for each villa — hand to the designer/printer.
Star Reference,"The meaning, planet, figure and symbol behind each star. For training & future reference (the why, per room)."
THE SIX LAYERS (and why each),
1 · Element,Fire / Earth / Air / Water — the star's core quality; the single idea each room is built around.
2 · Colour,"The visible expression of the element — a wall accent, textile or art. (We already theme by colour; this keeps it, now with an exact hex.)"
3 · Lucky stone,"A small polished display stone, as a keepsake touch. We use the affordable SUBSTITUTE gem (uparatna), never the precious one."
4 · Star tree,"The villa's tree from the Nakshatra Vana ('grove of stars') tradition — planted near the villa or potted. Most are native to these hills, so planting is authentic and easy."
5 · Symbol motif,"The star's emblem, carved / etched / embroidered on a door plaque, headboard or cushion. We use the SYMBOL, not religious imagery (see rules)."
6 · Aroma,"A signature scent diffused in the room, chosen by element. May use our own Ayurvedic oils."
+ Welcome card,A card in each room naming the star and its meaning — see the Welcome Cards tab.
DESIGN & TONE RULES (important),
"Keep it cultural, not religious","Our guests are global (Europe and beyond). In the rooms, favour the star's SYMBOL and nature over deity imagery. The presiding figures are listed in Star Reference for OUR understanding only — never for guest-facing display or any worship framing."
"A keepsake, never a claim","Guest-facing wording is a keepsake, never a prediction or a remedy. This programme is SEPARATE from our clinical / NABH / GST collateral and must never blur into a medical claim."
"One treatment, varied content","Every room gets the same treatment — plaque + motif + stone + scent + card + tree. Only the content changes by star, so it stays coherent and easy to roll out."
The two Maharaja Suites,"Shivalaya (1001) and Brahmalaya (1002) are named in the classical tradition, not after a star. Give them an elevated version of the same treatment, without a nakshatra."
FRONT-DESK NOTE,
Matching a guest's star,"A guest's own birth star (janma nakshatra) needs their date, exact time and place of birth. Many already know it; if not, our astrologer can compute it. Villas are matched to a guest's zodiac sign. (This is about matching guests — not the room theming itself.)"
SOURCES (for training / credibility),
Star trees,Sringeri Sharada Peetham Nakshatravana — a documented grove drawing on Western Ghats medicinal flora. (en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nakshatravana)
Lucky stones,The navaratna system — each planet's gem; the substitutes are the traditional uparatna (accepted lower-cost stones).
Colours,The classical (Prashna Marga-style) nakshatra palette. Confirm against our final décor.
Elements,"The zodiac sign each star occupies; nine boundary stars are shown with both signs (e.g., Mrigashira: Taurus–Gemini)."
TO CONFIRM BEFORE ROLL-OUT,
For the ED / advisors,(1) Colour palette + the boundary-star element calls — sanity-check with our Jyotish advisor. (2) Tree placement — horticulture to confirm by mature size & villa space. (3) Decide plant-at-villa vs a single signposted grove for the very large trees.
