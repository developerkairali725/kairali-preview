## 1. Bottom line — three decisions for FY26

The ledger says two things at once: **your single biggest B2B relationship just proved how fragile it is**, and **your portfolio quietly diversified to cover the gap**. Make that diversification deliberate.

1. **De-risk the Fit Reisen Group dependency — #1 priority.** One parent group is 42% of all B2B revenue and 77% of DACH. The FY25-26 airspace shock showed what a single point of failure costs. Defend the relationship in person, and in parallel build 2–3 independent DACH/Swiss agencies toward ~15% share each so no one partner can swing the book again.
2. **Fund France and GCC as the two growth bets.** France carries your highest quality per booking; GCC has your fastest growth. These earn the marginal travel and co-marketing budget this year — not the long tail.
3. **Formalise the India inside-sales engine.** India is now your #1 B2B market and still rising, but it is won through relationship management and CRM discipline (the in-house farming/hunting split), not international travel. Resource it as an inside-sales motion.

## 2. Does the B2B match the B2C MOA?

Short answer: **yes — the five-MOA structure holds.** DACH (M4a), France (M4b) and India (M1/M2) are exactly the markets carrying the agent book. The B2B lens forces three refinements to the priorities, not a rewrite of the framework.

|---|---|---|
| M4a — DACH end-user via specialist agency | 54% of all B2B; but 77% of it is one parent (Fit Reisen Group), which fell -75% YoY on the airspace shock. | CONFIRMED + at risk |
| M4b — France end-user via French agency | Highest avg ticket (₹3.57L) and rev/night (₹40,978) of any market; growing; understated by mis-tagging. | CONFIRMED — upgrade |

| M3 — NRI diaspora | Largely absent from agent ledger — NRIs book direct or via India DMCs. Not a B2B-visible MOA. | N/A in B2B |
| M6 — GCC (parked to 2027) | Saudi +160% YoY (₹5.5L→₹14.2L) on Jarir + Al-Muayed. Growing faster than the park decision assumed. | PULL FORWARD |

**The three refinements the B2B data forces:**
1. **Promote France from "emerging" to priority new-market.** It is your best market per booking and the number you have is too low — Absara, LPTI, Namaskar Inde and Far Beyond are French operators sitting in the "India" column.
2. **Un-park GCC.** +160% in a single year is not a 2027 problem. Run a 2026 test before the window closes.
3. **The real B2B question isn't a MOA at all — it's concentration.** 42% of your book on one parent group is the thing that can hurt you. It belongs at the top of the strategy, above any market-selection call.

### 3.3 The concentration risk — read this twice

**Fit Reisen Group = your single point of failure.** ₹2.94 Cr / 42% of all 3-FY B2B revenue. 43% of all bookings. 77% of DACH. Fit Reisen + Lotus Travel + AyTour + SpaDreams are one parent. "Germany," as a market, is effectively this one wholesaler. When their guests couldn't fly the Iran/GCC corridor in FY25-26, their volume dropped -75% and dragged your whole book down 19%. **This is recoverable (routes, not relationship) — but you cannot let one partner hold this much of the book again.**

## 5. Marketing spend & focus

One principle: **defend the base, fund the growth, ignore the tail.**

| Market | Spend posture | Where the rupees go |
|---|---|---|
| DACH | Defend — hold, don't grow demand-gen | In-person retention + co-op marketing with FRG; recruitment incentives for 2–3 independents. The constraint is routes, not demand — relationship spend, not ad spend. |
| France | Step up — biggest marginal increase | French-language clinical collateral; specialist-agency co-op; IFTM presence. Highest yield per booking justifies over-weighting. |
| GCC | Test budget — contained, time-boxed | ATM Dubai stand; Jarir/Al-Muayed enablement; halal + physician-led assets. Cap it; expand only on results. |
| India | Sustain — operational, not campaign | CRM + inside-sales tooling for the agent desk (LeadSquared attribution, call tracking). No travel line. |
| Long tail | Zero dedicated spend | Existing English digital infrastructure absorbs them. Do not build campaigns for "everywhere else." |

**If you only move money one way this year:** shift the marginal marketing rupee from the DACH long tail and one-off geographies into France (quality) and GCC (momentum) — while keeping DACH *relationship* spend intact to protect the 42%.

- **Country tagging is inconsistent.** Absara Voyages appears as both France and India; Namaskar Inde (French) is tagged France; GO India is tagged Spain. France is materially understated and India is slightly overstated as a result. Top-3 markets are safe; ranks 4–9 are directional only.
- **FY25-26 has 6 bookings with amounts still TBC/pending** (5 German). This nudges Germany up a little — but the booking count itself halved (61 → 28), so the DACH drop is real, not a settlement artifact. Cause is airspace, already diagnosed.
- **The "Anupam Summary" tab has a broken "Others / Int'l" row** (negative values from a formula error) — don't cite that line. The Clean FY sheets are the authoritative source and what this memo uses.
- **This is the B2B slice only.** NRI (M3) and India-direct guests largely bypass agents, so they're under-represented here. Read alongside the 2026 arrivals/PMS view for the full picture.

**One fix worth doing:** split the country column into *Agent Domicile* vs *Guest Origin*. Right now they're fused, which is exactly what hides France. A two-hour clean-up would make next year's version decision-grade.
