## Assignment rule (verbatim logic from the guide)
1. The janma nakshatra is the lunar mansion (one of 27, each 13°20') the Moon occupied at the exact moment of birth, read from the sidereal Moon position (explicitly distinct from Western sun-signs).
2. Inputs required: date of birth + exact time of birth (Moon changes star roughly every 24 h) + place of birth.
3. Discovery routes offered to guests: (a) many already know their star; (b) give birth details to the front desk and the resident Vedic astrologer identifies it; (c) any janma-nakshatra calculator or panchang.
4. The guest then finds their star in the reference table — each star names exactly one villa (Bharani names two: 201 & 203). Element follows the zodiac sign; the guide claims nine nakshatras straddle two signs but the table marks only three (Mrigashira, Chitra, Dhanishta).
5. Framing is affinity-based, not a booking guarantee: 'Your villa carries a star too — look it up to discover the meaning woven into your stay.' Closing disclaimer: 'not a prediction or a prescription.'
6. Suites 1001/1002 sit outside the star system entirely (named for Shiva/Brahma).
