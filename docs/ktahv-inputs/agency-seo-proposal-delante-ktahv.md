## Concise Summary

  - The SALT.agency document is a KTAHV‑specific SEO and content transformation proposal focused on technical SEO, schema, keyword mapping, GEO/AI search readiness, and large‑scale human content rewrite for ayurvedichealingvillage.com.
  - It is tailored to your current site state: \~376 live pages, heavy AI‑generated content on core treatment and retreat pages, broken international language architecture (/fr, /fr-fr, /de, /ru), and multiple technical SEO/security issues.
  - Key strength: compared to a generic template, SALT provides a deep page‑category audit, prioritised rewrite scope (priority vs full English vs international), and a dedicated AI search/GEO workstream aligned to Google AI Overviews and generative engines.
  - Red flag: the document remains largely “foundation‑only” — it defines work, hours, and costs but does not state outcome KPIs (leads/bookings), analytics stack, or measurement model; reporting is explicitly “to be clarified”.
  - Critical gap: international and multilingual work is acknowledged but not yet committed — language strategy, target countries, hreflang plan, and non‑English content workflows are deferred as a separate add‑on once open questions are answered.
  - Structural concern: this is an audit + ballpark estimate, not a full digital marketing retainer; there is no channel mix beyond organic search and content, no CRM/lead‑handling, and no explicit clinical review or health‑compliance process.
