1.2 The heritage-flagship strategic context

Kalari Kovilakom is the original Ayurveda property of CGH Earth — the 2004 entry that established CGH Earth Ayurveda as a sub-brand and gave the umbrella its credibility anchor. Nine years later, Kalari Rasayana opened (2013) as the second facility — the modern-clinical sibling absorbing demand overflow with a different terrain (lakeside vs palace), a different price point (broadly comparable but with a more accessible feel), and a different doctor-led credibility narrative (Dr Firoz Varun's 4th-generation lineage rather than palace heritage).

The strategic logic of the two-property structure within CGH Earth Ayurveda differs materially from the cluster competitors profiled earlier. Where the Mathew brothers operate two adjacent beachfront properties (Polly Mathew Somatheeram + Samana; Baby Mathews Somatheeram + Manaltheeram) within the same town through different operating entities, CGH Earth operates Kovilakom and Rasayana through one entity at deliberately different geographies (North Kerala / Palakkad vs South Kerala / Kollam) with deliberately different architectural-positioning equity (heritage palace vs modern lakeside). The CGH Earth strategy is portfolio differentiation at the property-architecture level, not geographic adjacency.
