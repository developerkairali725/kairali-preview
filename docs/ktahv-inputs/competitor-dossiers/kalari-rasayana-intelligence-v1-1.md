1.2 The CGH Earth Ayurveda umbrella — Kalari Rasayana's strategic context

Kalari Rasayana is not operated as a stand-alone property. It sits inside the CGH Earth Ayurveda sub-brand of the broader CGH Earth (Casino Group of Hotels) family enterprise. The umbrella architecture is materially different from the Mathew-brothers' two-property cluster model documented in Baby Mathews v1.1 / Manaltheeram v1.0 — there, two physically adjacent properties shared one operating company and identical 9-package catalogues. Here, the corporate parent is a 67-year-old hospitality group founded in 1957, owned by the Dominic family of six brothers, with award-decorated public leadership in Jose Dominic and a brand line — "Clean, Green, Healthy Earth" — that anchors a sustainability-first positioning.

Within CGH Earth Ayurveda specifically, only two properties operate as Ayurveda hospitals: Kalari Kovilakom (heritage palace, 2004) and Kalari Rasayana (lake-side modern build, 2013). The two Kalari properties share a common Ayurveda philosophy, joint awards (FICCI Best Medical Value Travel Ayurveda 2022–2023), and a unified umbrella site at cghearthayurveda.com — but they are operationally distinct, with separate Chief Medical Officers (Dr Jouhar Kanjhirala at Kovilakom, Dr Firoz Varun at Rasayana) and different architectural and clinical-aesthetic positioning. Kovilakom is the heritage flagship; Rasayana is the modern-clinical sibling. Detailed CGH Earth corporate context is in Appendix A.
