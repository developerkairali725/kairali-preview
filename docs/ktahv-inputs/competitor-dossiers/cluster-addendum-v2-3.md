Category B — Methodology / Discipline Lessons

Three workflow findings emerged during the Soukya build that improve the reliability of all future research rounds, including any v2.4 refresh of the prior 8 locked files. These are documented here so they can be applied at the next research cycle.

B.1 Property-hosted PDFs are systematically under-utilised in Perplexity output

|  |
| :-: |
| Methodology finding — applies to all v2.4 refreshes \| Soukya's three most material primary-source findings — the GoI Best Wellness Centre Award, the AYUSH Centre of Excellence designation, and the case studies + TRI research collaboration — were ALL sitting in open-access PDFs and HTML pages on soukya.com/images/ and soukya.com/casestudies.php that Perplexity Round 1 and Round 2 did not surface. The pattern is clear: research-output tools systematically under-weight property-hosted PDFs (often dated material, often under /images/ paths) and secondary navigation pages (case studies, research findings, awards). The v2.4 research workflow should explicitly require: (a) sitemap.xml fetch for each property; (b) directory walk of /images/ and /pdf/ paths; (c) explicit fetch of case-studies, research, and awards pages from each property's secondary navigation. These are now considered required steps, not nice-to-have. |

B.2 Two-tier pricing structure (INR vs USD) is the cluster norm, not the exception

Soukya's brochure 2014 explicitly states 'Indian rates applicable only for Indian National Permanent Resident residing in India.' Foreign-passport-holding NRIs and OCI-card holders pay USD rates. The INR/USD ratio across categories is non-linear: standard rooms run at INR 61/USD (close to current FX) but Presidential Suite runs at INR 45/USD — meaningfully foreign-buyer-favourable. This is consistent with the international-CEO and Royal Family patronage narrative. The applicable v2.4 review task: re-check whether the same two-tier non-linear pricing structure applies at Amal Tamara, Kovilakom, Carnoustie, Somatheeram and Manaltheeram. If yes, this is a cluster-wide pricing-architecture pattern that should be reflected in the master Treatment Programmes & Tariff Structure reference (currently v2.2).

B.3 Dated rate cards are operational debt, not historical artefact

|  |
| :-: |
| Discipline finding for KTAHV's own published tariff \| Soukya's e-brochure with full tariff page is explicitly dated 'Tariffs - 2014.' Eleven years later, that same brochure is still hosted on the property's own domain and still propagates to Scribd-indexed search results. Cross-referenced against actual transaction prices (Cotswold Life 2019, Ayuruniverse 2024), the brochure floor of USD 195/pp/night was already five years out of date by 2019, and current transaction prices have moved 6× above the brochure's programme rates. This is operational debt: partner agencies are pricing on a different sheet from the property's own published tariff. For Kairali specifically: KTAHV's published tariff sheet should be (a) explicitly version-stamped, (b) revised annually as a hard discipline, and (c) removed/withdrawn from public access if it cannot be revised on schedule. A stale rate card hosted on the property's own site is a weak-distribution-discipline signal to international buyer agents. |
