1.2 What Somatheeram is, in one sentence

Somatheeram is a 6-package, 6-villa-category beach-front Ayurveda resort that has merchandised the same six generic Ayurveda concepts (Rejuvenation, Body Purification, Stress Management, Slimming, Body Immunisation, Beauty Care) for at least a decade, with tightly disciplined seasonal pricing, transparent inclusions, and a strong European distribution moat.

1.3 Where Somatheeram sits competitively

Somatheeram is Kairali's nearest direct competitor on three dimensions:

Geography: both are Kerala Ayurveda properties serving European guests as the primary international market.

Clinical-Ayurveda framing: both position themselves as treatment-led rather than spa-led — Somatheeram explicitly states it serves "only treatment patients" and that room-only bookings are not allowed without a package.

Award and accreditation language: both use government-recognized credentials prominently (Somatheeram: 10x Best Ayurvedic Centre Kerala Government; Kairali: NABH accreditation).

Where they diverge:

Setting: Somatheeram is beachfront / Chowara cliff-top; Kairali is rural / Palakkad. Different guest physical experience.

Catalogue depth: Somatheeram has 6 packages; Kairali v2.2 has 27 treatment programmes + 3 yoga + 5 training = 35 total.

Accreditation tier: Somatheeram has government-of-Kerala awards; Kairali has NABH accreditation (the gold standard for hospital quality in India). NABH \> tourism-board awards on a clinical credibility hierarchy.

Doctor visibility: Somatheeram does not name doctors on its public package pages; Kairali v2.2 names four BAMS doctors with credentials, languages, focus areas.

Pharma: Somatheeram does not have an in-house WHO GMP pharma unit. Kairali has KAPPL.

Section 3 — Pricing architecture (full tariff)

Somatheeram operates two regional tariffs in parallel — one for European countries (EUR-denominated) and one for North America, Asia, Middle East, and most other regions (USD-denominated). The EUR tariff is approximately 13% lower than a straight USD-to-EUR conversion of the USD tariff. This is a deliberate distribution-channel decision: Europe is their primary international market and they price competitively against German Ayurveda alternatives there.

3.1 Pricing model summary

Pricing is per person per package (not room-night).

Three seasons: Ayurveda Season (Jun–Sept, lowest), High Season (Oct–Nov & Apr–May in EUR / Oct–19 Dec & Apr–May in USD), Peak Season (Dec–Mar in EUR / 20 Dec–Mar in USD).

Pricing tables exist for each package × each season × six villa categories × single/double occupancy.

Slimming and Body Immunisation are tariffed at the 28-night package rate but bookable at 21-night minimum. The other four packages are tariffed at 14-night rates.

Room-only rates exist as a separate tariff but cannot be booked standalone — only in connection with a package booking.

All applicable taxes are included in stated rates.
