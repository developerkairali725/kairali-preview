**About Your Star**

A *nakshatra* (नक्षत्र) is one of the 27 'lunar mansions' — the resting points the Moon passes through as it crosses the sky. The zodiac is divided into 27 of them, each 13°20ʹ wide, and the Moon travels through about one a day. Every villa here is named after one of these stars.

**Finding your villa's star**

The two tables in this booklet are arranged by **room number**. Look for the number on your key in the left-hand column, and read across to find your villa's star — its element, colour, lucky stone, tree and qualities. Each star also has its own tree, drawn from the old *Nakshatra Vana* ('grove of stars') tradition, many of them native to these very hills.

**Knowing your own birth star**

Your personal star is set by three details — your **date of birth**, **exact time of birth** and **place of birth** — because the Moon changes star roughly once a day. Many guests already know theirs; if you don't, share those three details at our front desk and we will gladly work it out for you.

A note: the star is read from the *sidereal* sky, so it may differ from the sun-sign you know from Western horoscopes — they are two different systems.
