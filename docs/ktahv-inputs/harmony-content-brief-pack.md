# Harmony Video Library — Content Brief Pack

*Prompts & Guardrails for Vikash and the Writer. For every content and build task in the action plan: a copy-paste AI prompt plus a DO / DON'T guardrail, grounded in the MOA cohorts, the European buyer journey, and Kairali compliance rules.*

## How to use this pack

Each task has three parts: (1) a Prompt with [SQUARE BRACKETS] to fill in; (2) a DO / DON'T table of the mistakes that matter for Kairali specifically; (3) the owner. Run the prompt, then check the output against the DON'T column before anything goes live. Nothing publishes until it passes the compliance check in Section 9. Read Section 0 first — the shared context every prompt assumes.

## 0. Shared context every prompt assumes

### The five MOA cohorts (who we are speaking to)

- **DA1–DA3 (DACH end-user):** German-speaking, ~74% female, often solo, 14+ night stays, catalogue/agency-mediated, long lead time, plans carefully. DACH volume is currently down — rebuilding trust matters.
- **FR1–FR3 (France end-user):** French traveller, wellness-curious, highest average ticket in the ledger, channel fragmented across smaller agencies, growing. FR2 is a group-booking sub-persona.
- **India Male / India Female:** domestic premium, faster decision (11-day median), multi-channel, executive skew on the male side.
- NRI Diaspora and B2B European specialist partners exist but are not the primary target for this public video content.

### The buyer-journey moments this content serves

- **M1 — the 90-second website first impression (Stage 4).** Public video + page is often the FIRST trust contact. It must read as credible and calm, not salesy.
- **M2 — the pre-booking physician video consultation (Stage 5).** Content should make the practice feel clinically grounded and human, nudging toward an enquiry, never hard-selling.
- The principle throughout: **AI prepares, human delivers.** Content warms and informs; a named human closes. No content should try to hard-close a booking.

### Brand disambiguation (a frequent, costly mistake)

KTAHV = the 65-acre NABH-accredited clinical healing village in Palakkad. The 118-year heritage belongs to the Kairali GROUP, founded 1908 — not to any single product or to the book. Gita Ramesh is the published author and brand authority. Harmony is her book. Never imply the book or a video is 118 years old; never attach clinical/medical claims to anything but KTAHV's actual accredited services.

### Banned words (do not use in any copy)

bespoke, curated, world-class, discerning, profound, meticulous(ly), exquisite, unparalleled, transformative, life-changing, magical, pampering, unlock, journey (as filler), holistic (as filler), luxury (as lead descriptor), award-winning (unless a specific named award follows).

### Compliance — the health-claim line

This is the single most important rule and ties to the live GST/healthcare-positioning matter. Frame practices as TRADITIONALLY done for a purpose, not as medical treatments that cure or fix. Say "traditionally practised to support calm" — not "cures anxiety." Say "may help you feel grounded" — not "balances blood sugar / treats insomnia." No diagnosis, no cure, no medical outcome promises. When unsure, soften.

## 9. The compliance gate — run on EVERYTHING before publishing — Owner: Anupam + Abilash (final sign-off)

The non-negotiable last step for any copy produced above. Protects the healthcare positioning that matters for the GST matter.

**Prompt:** You are a compliance reviewer for Kairali Ayurvedic Group content. Review the draft and flag EVERY issue in: (1) MEDICAL CLAIMS — any wording implying cure/treat/fix/heal/diagnose or promising a medical outcome; rewrite into 'traditionally supports'/'may help you feel' framing. (2) BANNED WORDS — bespoke, curated, world-class, discerning, profound, meticulous(ly), exquisite, unparalleled, transformative, life-changing, magical, pampering, unlock, journey (filler), holistic (filler), luxury (as lead), award-winning (no named award). (3) BRAND ERRORS — implying the book/video is 118 years old (heritage = Kairali GROUP since 1908) or attaching clinical/NABH claims to anything other than KTAHV's real accredited services. (4) TONE — hard-sell closes, sentences over 25 words, adjective-stacking with no specific fact. Output a two-column table ISSUE (quoted) | FIX (rewritten), then a verdict: PASS / NEEDS REVISION / RESTART.

**Guardrail — DO:** run on every piece before it goes live; treat NEEDS REVISION/RESTART as blocking; log recurring issues and fix them at the source. **DON'T:** skip it because copy 'looks fine'; publish with an unresolved medical claim; override a fail to hit a deadline.

*If a piece fails the gate twice for the same reason, the problem is upstream — fix the originating prompt in this pack, not just the draft.*
