# KTAHV · Marketing Action Points — MOA-Driven

*A detailed, channel-by-channel marketing plan built directly from the five-MOA framework and the FY26 B2B findings.*

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Owner | Anupam (Head of Marketing) + Vikash (Digital) + Ambuj (AI/Tech) |
| From | Abilash K. Ramesh, Executive Director |
| Date | 23 June 2026 |
| Basis | MOA Matrix v3.0 · 3-FY B2B ledger · existing martech stack |

**The discipline that governs everything below:** Clinical / GST framing is mandatory on every asset, every channel, every MOA. KTAHV is a NABH-accredited Ayurvedic hospital under an active GST SCN. The vocabulary is patient · physician (BAMS) · treatment · programme · therapy · panchakarma. Never spa, resort, holiday, getaway, pampering, luxury escape. This isn't tone — it's tax and accreditation exposure.

## 1. The strategic picture in one page

- **India (M1 + M2) is the rising volume engine** (+45% YoY, now #1) and the only group marketing can acquire directly at scale.
- **France (M4b) is the highest-quality market** (₹3.57L avg ticket) and is under-built — the best ROI on new spend.
- **DACH (M4a) demand is intact but route-constrained** (the airspace shock), and 42% of all B2B sits on one agent group — so marketing's DACH job is recovery support + demand that *any* agent can fulfil, reducing single-agent dependency.
- **NRI (M3) is high-LTV and direct-addressable;** GCC (M6) is un-parking on +160% growth.

## 3. Cross-cutting action points (apply across all MOAs)

### 3.1 Fix attribution — the foundation everything sits on
- **Call tracking (Phase 1, Exotel/Ozonetel):** dynamic numbers on every landing page — parents-care, NRI and GCC buyers phone, and those conversions are invisible today.
- **LeadSquared as system of record:** every enquiry stamped source/campaign/MOA; cost-per-qualified-lead replaces cost-per-click as the headline metric.
- **Conversion values by MOA fed to bidding:** so spend optimises to revenue, not volume (France/NRI/DACH outrank a short India break).
- **AI lead-classification (v4.3) in the loop:** auto-score and route enquiries by intent and MOA.

### 3.2 GEO / AEO — get cited in AI answers (the new frontier)
- **Why now:** buyers increasingly ask AI assistants "best Ayurvedic hospital in Kerala / NABH panchakarma centre". Being the cited answer is the new top-of-funnel.
- **Track it:** stand up Profound / Otterly.ai / Peec AI to monitor where Kairali is (and isn't) cited across AI engines, by query and market.
- **Feed it:** structured, citable clinical content — schema (MedicalBusiness + LocalBusiness + AggregateRating), authoritative FAQ, physician-authored explainers, the Wikipedia page, Gita Ramesh authority content.
- **Measure:** share of AI citations for priority queries as a standing KPI alongside organic rank.

### 3.3 Conversational layer — route the bots by MOA & language
- Priya (pre-sales): tuned per MOA — India intent, NRI parents-care, French/German/Arabic handoffs; first responder on Search/Meta/WhatsApp clicks.
- Ria (in-property): on-site guest experience, upsell of programmes, women's-wellness queries.
- K-Serve (outbound voice): reactivation of dormant enquiries and B2C follow-up; coordinate with the CRM cadence so it doesn't collide with the partner desk.

### 3.4 Landing-page system (one architecture, many spokes)
- Organic hub /ayurvedic-detox-kerala/ (indexed, E-E-A-T, schema) — never receives paid traffic.
- Paid hub /detox-rejuvenation-program/ (noindex) + message-matched spokes per ad group and language (stress, sleep, women's-wellness, panchakarma/DACH, france, gcc).
- French and German spokes are full native localisations; no IP auto-redirect; correct currency and compliance (DSGVO for DE).

### 3.5 De-risk the B2B concentration from the demand side
- **Marketing's role in the 42% problem:** generate qualified DACH/France/GCC demand that flows to multiple partners, not one — lookalike campaigns seeded from agency bookers, co-op assets every independent can deploy. As the partner base widens, single-agent dependency falls.

### 3.6 Content & PR engine
- Run the "Why Ayurveda" autonomous content engine to keep clinical, citable content flowing (feeds SEO + GEO/AEO + social).
- PR by market: extend the German-market PR playbook to France; keep Gita Ramesh central as founder-authority across earned media.
- Every output passes the clinical/GST language gate before publish.
