# Agency Capability and Specialist Workstream Map - 2026

A high-end engagement may require multiple specialist capabilities. One person or AI may coordinate them, but the operating system must not pretend expertise or approval authority that is absent.

| Capability | Core outputs | When specialist is mandatory or strongly advised |
|---|---|---|
| Business/market strategy | Economics, market definition, positioning, growth choices, portfolio priorities | Material market entry, pricing/offer change, major capital allocation |
| Competitive intelligence | Market map, competitor evidence, SOV, opportunity model, attack plan | Market-share or multi-channel strategic engagement |
| Technical SEO | Crawls, rendering, logs, architecture, migration, indexing | Large/dynamic/international site, migration, traffic loss |
| Content strategy/editorial | Intent map, content model, briefs, governance, expert evidence | YMYL, large content programme, multilingual publishing |
| Digital PR/link/reputation | Linkable assets, media, mentions, review/reputation | Authority gap, brand discovery, reputation risk |
| UX research/service design | Interviews, tasks, journeys, prototypes, research repository | Complex journeys, high-value B2B/health/travel/checkout |
| UI/design system | Components, responsive behavior, visual hierarchy, accessibility | New/rebuilt front end or premium/luxury brand |
| CRO/experimentation | Research, hypotheses, test design, statistical governance | Meaningful traffic and repeated conversion optimization |
| Analytics/data engineering | Tracking plan, warehouse, identity, attribution, dashboards | Multi-domain/region, CRM/offline revenue, complex ecommerce |
| Development/architecture | Frontend/backend, CMS, APIs, performance, reliability | Custom build, replatform, integrations, high scale |
| QA/DevOps/SRE | Automated tests, CI/CD, rollback, monitoring, incidents | Any revenue-critical release; mandatory for migrations |
| Accessibility | WCAG audit, assistive-tech testing, remediation validation | Legal/contractual risk or critical public/customer service |
| Security/privacy/legal | Threat model, testing, consent, claims, contracts, data governance | Sensitive data, regulated claims, payments, international markets |
| Localization/transcreation | Glossary, translation, local SEO, linguistic/functional QA | Every new language/market |
| Local SEO/reputation | Profiles, rank grids, citations, reviews, location pages | Physical locations or service areas |
| Merchant/feed/marketplace | Catalog, feeds, diagnostics, shopping, marketplace operations | D2C/Shopify/product-led acquisition |
| CRM/lifecycle/sales ops | Routing, nurture, service SLA, retention, cohort value | B2B pipeline, repeat purchase, booking/consultation |
| Clinical/legal subject expert | Claims, evidence, safety, review and sign-off | Health, medical, legal, finance or other high-impact content |

## AI role

AI may accelerate research, extraction, comparison, issue drafting, code generation, test generation and report assembly. It does not replace accountable domain experts, user research participants, legal/clinical approval, security testing or independent release authorization. Each AI output must retain sources, evidence labels and uncertainty.
