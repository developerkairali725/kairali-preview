## 2 · What the competitor sites do (SEO/structure signals)

- **Title-tag pattern:** "Top/Best Bridal & Wedding Makeup Artist in Delhi NCR |
  <Name>" (Guneet Virdi verbatim). They all chase "bridal makeup artist in Delhi"
  head terms + city-intent long tail. Local-SEO listicles (kahanimakeover,
  sloshout, fareenas) dominate the SERP around them — every top artist is farmed
  into "top 10 Delhi MUA" content that links to IG first, site second.
- **Structure (Guneet Virdi, fetched):** Home (hero + why-choose-us + services
  narrative) · Services · Studio · Videos · Contact. Booking = enquiry form +
  phone; deposit/cancellation policy stated on-page.
- **Several comps are JS-heavy/slow or plain broken** (parulgarg.com NXDOMAIN —
  she runs on IG + aggregators; chandnisingh.com renders empty without JS).
  A fast, crawlable, schema-complete site is an easy technical win in this niche.
- **Aggregator dependence:** WedMeGood/WeddingWire storefronts carry the
  portfolio + review weight (200+ photos noted for Guneet). The personal site's
  job is brand control + direct enquiry, not marketplace replacement.
- **Education arms are standard at the top:** Chandni Singh academy, Meenakshi
  Dutt academy, IDeA/college tie-ins. Sneha's Blush School of Beauty needs a
  visible home on the site — it is both revenue and authority.

## 3 · Practitioner insight — what converts in this niche (2026 consensus)

- **Instagram discovers, Google captures intent, WhatsApp closes.** The funnel
  is IG reel → profile → site/link → WhatsApp chat. The site must make the
  IG→WhatsApp handoff frictionless, not replace it (kolkatadigital, adwordsify,
  glossgenius practitioner guides).
- **WhatsApp-first CTA** is the single highest-leverage element — "most brides
  reach out on WhatsApp before they call or email." Persistent, thumb-zone
  placement (our own binding law: docs/design/chat-bubble-placement.md).
- **Portfolio diversity beats volume:** 20–50 best images showing range
  (bridal/cocktail/minimal/editorial), organized by occasion; before/after and
  process content builds trust.
- **Specific testimonials with names/venues** out-convert star ratings.
- **Transparent starting prices** ("starting ₹X") filter serious enquiries —
  BUT we hold NO verified Blush Lounge prices; price-on-enquiry until Sneha
  supplies figures (HARD GATE 6 — never invent).
- **Booking mechanics** at the top tier: enquiry form + deposit norms (50%,
  6-month lead) — an "enquire for your date" date-picker form matches how brides
  actually buy; instant-calendar booking (Setmore/GlossGenius) is a
  freelancer-tier pattern, not a premium-bridal one.
- **Blogs only if maintained** — a stale blog hurts (the old site's 2-post 2019
  blog is evidence in our own mine). Drop-or-rewrite, never carry dormant.
