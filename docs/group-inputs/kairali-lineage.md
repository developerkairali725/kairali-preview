## 2 · The four live numbers, resolved

**The hypothesis holds: all four are true of *different things*, and every one of them is published without its referent.**

### 2.1 — **1908**
- **What it's actually true of:** *Nothing that has been evidenced.* It is presented as the origin of the **family's Ayurvedic practice / lineage**, not of any company. No registry entity exists from 1908. No independent (non-Kairali) source confirms 1908 — and the one press source that discusses the origin says **1909**, not 1908.
- **Where it appears now:** kairali.com footer sitewide ("© Kairali Ayurvedic Group Since 1908."), kairali.com/about-us.aspx, kairali.com/kairali-group-profile.aspx ("Kairali was formed in the year 1908"), kappl.co.in footer sitewide, the logo tagline "health through ayurveda since 1908" on KTAHV letterheads/rate cards and product price lists, and **our own sites** ("Kairali Ayurvedic Group — Since 1908").
- **Defensible?** **NOT as published.** "Kairali Ayurvedic Group … Since 1908" is false on the group's own evidence: the Group's co-founder says the Group was established in **1989**, and KAPPL's own About page says the same. 1908 is defensible **only** as a *family-lineage* claim, and even then it is a Kairali-only assertion with a competing 1909 in the record. **This is the conflation the brief suspected, and it is confirmed.**

### 2.2 — **1989**
- **What it's actually true of:** **The founding of the Kairali Ayurvedic Group** — the first Kairali Ayurvedic Centre, New Delhi, founded by K.V. Ramesh and Gita Ramesh.
- **Where it appears now:** kappl.co.in/company-profile.aspx and /about-us.aspx, ayurvedichealingvillage.com/kairali-ayurvedic-group/, kairali.com (as "Training centre"), the KTAHV profile PDF, Gita Ramesh's interview, Abishek Ramesh's franchiseindia interview.
- **Defensible?** **YES — this is the strongest-evidenced date in the entire record**, stated by the co-founder herself and corroborated by four further Kairali sources plus 2026 trade press. Caveat: it is a **business-founding** date, not an incorporation date — no company was registered in 1989 (the oldest active one is 1996). Say "founded", never "incorporated".

### 2.3 — **"115+ years"**
- **What it's actually true of:** It is a **rolling counter off 1908** (1908 + 115 = 2023). It therefore inherits every problem 1908 has — *and* it is **stale** (2026 − 1908 = 118).
- **Where it appears now:** ayurvedichealingvillage.com homepage ("115+ YEARS OF EXPERIENCE"), /panchkarma-treatment.html ("We are A 115+ Years Old Panchakarma treatment specialist"; "With a legacy of over 115 years, Kairali - The Ayurvedic Healing Village has been helping humankind…"; "Exceptional legacy of 115 years"), /treatment-for-bronchial-diseases/ ("115+ Years Heritage"). Sibling variants: "more than 111 years of experience" (kairaliproducts.com/pages/the-kairali-group — 1908+111 = 2019, stale by 7 years), "more than 100 years of experience… with 8 decades of modern day experience" (kappl.co.in), "a ten-decade old brand" (kairali.com/about-us.aspx).
- **Defensible?** **NO.** Three compounding faults: (1) it rests on the unevidenced 1908; (2) it is **attached to KTAHV**, an entity that opened in 1998/99 — a property that has existed ~27 years is described as "115+ Years Old"; (3) the family of counters (100/111/115) are hardcoded and drift out of date, proving no single source of truth. **On KTAHV pages this is also a clinical-register risk**, not just an accuracy one.

### 2.4 — **"25 years"**
- **What it's actually true of:** **KTAHV's own age**, counted from its 1999 opening (1999 + 25 = 2024). Also **stale** (2026 − 1999 = 27).
- **Where it appears now:** ayurvedichealingvillage.com **homepage** — "With a legacy of 25 years, our skilled therapists provide personalized therapies…"
- **Defensible?** **The referent is right; the number is stale and the page contradicts itself.** The single most damaging finding in this audit: **"With a legacy of 25 years" and "115+ YEARS OF EXPERIENCE" appear on the same homepage**, describing the same entity. Both were fetched from `https://ayurvedichealingvillage.com/` in one pass on 2026-07-16.

## 3 · Ruling recommendation — the locked fact

**Principle:** every "since" must **name its referent**. A bare "Kairali Ayurvedic Group — Since 1908" asserts a corporate age the group's own co-founder contradicts. The fix is not to delete the heritage — it is to attribute it to the family, and give each company its own true date.

**Blocking caveat:** 1908 itself is **not yet verified** (§4, §5). Options (a)–(d) below are the correct *structure* and should be adopted now; the **literal year 1908 must survive the family check in §5 before it ships**. If 1908 cannot be substantiated, the same wording works with the year replaced by whatever the family can evidence — or with the numeral dropped ("a family practising Ayurveda for four generations").

### (a) Group site — kairali.com
- **Ship:** **"Kairali Ayurvedic Group — founded 1989."**
- Heritage line, where the story is told: **"Founded in 1989 by K.V. Ramesh and Gita Ramesh, drawing on a family practising Ayurveda since 1908."**
- **Retire** the sitewide footer "© Kairali Ayurvedic Group Since 1908." → **"© 2026 Kairali Ayurvedic Group. Founded 1989."**
- **Retire** "Kairali was formed in the year 1908", "a ten-decade old brand", "Spreading Ayurveda since 1908" (unreferented).
- ⚠️ The agency build's footer **"© 1956-2026"** is a **fifth** number with **no support anywhere** — not on live kairali.com (live footer reads "Since 1908"), not on any registry, not in any Kairali source. **Do not propagate it.** Treat as an agency error to be struck.

### (b) KTAHV
- **Ship:** **"Kairali — The Ayurvedic Healing Village, established 1999."** *(pending the 1998/1999 ruling in §5 — do not ship a year until the family settles it)*
- Where lineage is relevant: **"Part of the Kairali Ayurvedic Group, founded 1989, from a family practising Ayurveda since 1908."**
- **Remove from every KTAHV page:** "115+ Years Old", "legacy of over 115 years", "115+ Years Heritage", "Exceptional legacy of 115 years", and the bare "legacy of 25 years". These attach a family-lineage age to a 1999 property.
- **Never hardcode a duration.** If a duration must appear, compute it from a dated constant, so it cannot go stale like 100/111/115 did.
- Operator entity for legal/clinical contexts: **Kairali Ayurvedic Health Resorts Private Limited** (CIN U55103DL1996PTC078294).

### (c) KAPPL / products
- **Ship:** **"Kairali Ayurvedic Products Private Limited — incorporated 2007. Ayurvedic manufacturing in the family since 1948."**
- This is the **cleanest true statement available** and it keeps real heritage: 1948 is well-evidenced across three independent Kairali sources including Gita Ramesh's own interview.
- **Retire** "more than 111 years of experience" and "more than 100 years of experience"; **retire** the kappl.co.in footer "© Kairali Ayurvedic Group Since 1908."
- ⚠️ **KAPPL's own page currently contradicts itself**: the body says "KAIRALI Ayurvedic Group was founded in 1989" while the footer says "Since 1908" — on the same page.
- **Spa Labs:** may continue as a **product brand/unit under KAPPL** (Kairali's own brochure lists it that way). **Never** describe Spa Labs Pvt Ltd as a current company — that entity is **struck off**. Never claim KAPPL "was formerly Spa Labs" (§4).

### (d) Logo tagline "health through ayurveda | since 1908"
- **This is the hardest case** — it is on printed letterheads, rate cards and product price lists, so it is expensive to change and is arguably read as a *brand/lineage* mark rather than a corporate-age claim.
- **Recommended:** keep the *tagline as a lockup* but stop it functioning as a standalone corporate claim. Wherever the group is **named as a company** next to it, the founding line must read **1989**.
- **Preferred wording if the lockup is ever reset:** **"health through ayurveda | a family tradition since 1908"** — one word ("family") converts an indefensible corporate claim into a defensible lineage claim, at minimal design cost.
- **Do not re-print anything carrying the bare 1908 lockup until §5 Q1 is answered.**
