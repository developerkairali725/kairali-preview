## 1. THE REAL MOA SEGMENTS (ranked)

The task brief's segment stub (`{"name":"a","who":"b"…}`) is a placeholder. The real, locked segments are the MOA Matrix v3.0 five-core set (plus the two B2B sub-MOAs and GCC). Ranked here by **where new website/marketing spend earns the most**, which is the priority order the two source docs actually argue — not by raw volume alone.

### Rank 1 — M1: India Domestic Male (self + parents-care) · ~35% · P1 Volume
- **Who:** Tier-1/Tier-2 metro Indian men, 40–60. Two intents in one MOA: booking a physician-led programme *for himself* (stress, metabolic, lifestyle, allopathic-failure case) **and** *for ageing parents* (joint care, recovery, preventive). High commercial intent; phones before booking; shorter premium stays at high nightly value.
- **Value:** The rising volume engine — India (M1+M2) is +45% YoY, now #1, and the **only** group marketing can acquire *directly at scale*.
- **Messaging:** Physician-supervised, NABH-accredited, protocol-driven. Two hooks: *"reset your health under medical supervision"* (self) and *"a supervised programme for your parents"* (parents-care). Clinical register only.

### Rank 2 — M2: India Domestic Female (self) · ~25% · P1 Volume
- **Who:** Indian women buying for themselves — stress/burnout, hormonal balance, postnatal recovery, sleep, weight/metabolic, menopause, autoimmune, chronic pain. Self-decision. Researches longer; responds to credibility + relatability.
- **Value:** Second half of the P1 volume engine. **Postnatal and hormonal programmes are differentiated, ownable territory** no competitor holds cleanly.
- **Messaging:** Women's-health-specific, physician-led, safe and evidence-respecting. Female-practitioner voices, myth-busting, day-in-the-programme — credible women's-health voices, *not* generic lifestyle influencers.
- **Data note:** Real India female-decision-maker share is **~34%**, not the ~65% legacy artefact (spouses arriving as Secondary rows inflate it). Count decisions, not arrivals.

### Rank 3 — M4b: France End-User via French Specialist Agency · ~2–5% · P2 Stable
- **Who:** French / French-speaking wellness-serious guests via French specialist operators; group tours common.
- **Value:** **The highest-quality market and the best ROI on new spend.** Highest average ticket in the entire portfolio (₹3.57L marketing-doc figure; ₹5.6L B2B avg ticket). Under-built and nearly invisible today because French operators are mis-tagged "India" in the ledger. Fund out of proportion to current headline revenue.
- **Messaging:** Native French (never machine translation), physician-led, authentic Kerala depth. Market keyword *"cure ayurvédique"* — **flag:** check against the FR banned-sense list before any KTAHV surface use ("cure" in the banned sense is locked out).

### Rank 4 — M4a: DACH End-User via European Specialist Agency · ~2–7% · P2 Under Review
- **Who:** German/Austrian/Swiss guests, woman 45–65 archetype, chronic stress/menopause, long (14+ night) Panchakarma via German-language agency. Historically the biggest international block.
- **Value:** Demand is **intact but route-constrained** (airspace/corridor shock). Marketing's job is recovery support + generating demand *any* agent can fulfil — because **42–43% of all B2B revenue depends on one parent, Fit Reisen Group** (Fit Reisen + Lotus Travel + AyTour + SpaDreams). Reducing that concentration is a standing strategic goal.
- **Messaging:** Authentic, physician-led, long-programme depth; DSGVO-compliant, formal *Sie*, Euro pricing, no IP auto-redirect.

### Rank 5 — M3: NRI Diaspora · ~10–12% · P3 Leverage (candidate for P2)
- **Who:** Punjabi / Gujarati / UP-NRI / South-Indian / Ismaili households, 2–3 decades in US/UK/Canada/Gulf/Singapore. High LTV, often bring parents, blend roots-reconnection with genuine clinical need.
- **Value:** **Marketing-owned and direct-addressable** — largely invisible in the B2B agent ledger, so attribution gains here show up as "found revenue". If consistent-classification growth is 30%+ YoY, promote to P2.
- **Messaging:** *"Bring your parents for a physician-led programme"* + reconnect-with-roots, kept clinical. NABH credibility reassures a diaspora used to Western healthcare standards.

### Rank 6 — M5a / M5b: B2B Partner-as-Customer · 26 + 23 partners · P2 Engine / P3 Support
- **Who:** M5a = the European specialist *agency itself* is the buyer (list / push / renew decision). M5b = Indian DMCs, yoga teachers, Ayurvedic doctors, retreat organisers, HNI concierges.
- **Value:** The B2B channel is a customer in its own right, not "just a channel" — 59 active parent-partners across 14 countries. The website serves them with trade-facing credibility assets (co-op material every independent can deploy), diversifying away from the Fit Reisen 42% dependency.
- **Messaging:** Institutional trust — NABH, physician masthead, programme depth, listing/enablement kits.

### Rank 7 — M6: GCC / Saudi · ~3–5% · Park to 2027 (live test)
- **Who:** Gulf (KSA/UAE) guests — privacy-conscious, family-oriented, premium; +160% growth through a small agent set.
- **Value:** Un-parking on momentum; capped time-boxed test, decision-gated on real CPA.
- **Messaging:** Physician-led, privacy/discretion, halal infrastructure, family-suite programmes. GCC handled now via culturally-adapted English; Arabic is Year 3+.

**Explicitly NOT MOAs (do not spend to acquire):** Caucasian-direct-Anglo yoga-curious one-trip travellers (one-and-done; service well via SEO/word-of-mouth, don't market); Corporate Wellness India (pricing mismatch; route to KAC at group level).

**Two fronts to win simultaneously:** (1) Western trust/lifestyle publishers wellness tourists *research through* (WebMD, Healthline, Mayo, Johns Hopkins, Goop, MindBodyGreen, Well+Good); (2) direct retreat competitors they *choose between* (Ananda, Six Senses Vana, COMO Shambhala, SHA, Vaidyagrama, Sitaram, Somatheeram).

## 4. KNOWLEDGE-BASE NOTE (the "why" layer)

`strategy/KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md` (v1.0, 2026-07-10) is the depth/rationale companion — the *why* behind the method, grounded in 2026 search-industry and AI-engineering research. It is a research synthesis, **NOT** Kairali business data: it contains **no entity-specific segments, prices, competitors, or facts**. Its reusable output is standards and architecture requirements. **It is explicitly time-sensitive — re-verify figures at each project start (P1.8); the landscape shifts monthly. All stats are attributed third-party industry sources, not Kairali's own.**

The load-bearing case for this programme: **build to be AI-*cited*, not just ranked.**
- **Search is now "be the answer," not "rank a list."** ~55–60% of searches are zero-click; AI Overviews appear in ~55% of searches; when one shows, #1 organic CTR drops ~58%.
- **The strategic wedge (why secondary specialist sites win):** traditional SEO ≠ AI citation. Google/AI source overlap fell from ~70% to **under 20%**; ~90% of ChatGPT citations come from pages **not in Google's top 20**. A smaller, specialist, well-structured site can win AI citations **without ranking #1** — first-mover advantage while competitors run 2022 playbooks.
- **AI-referred traffic is the best traffic** — +12% pages/visit, −23% bounce, **~3× conversion** vs normal organic. This is exactly the pre-qualified secondary-sales traffic the factory exists to capture.
- **What gets cited** (Princeton + Georgia Tech GEO study, up to 40% visibility lift): answer-first structure (~44% of LLM citations come from the first 30% of an article), **a statistic every ~150–200 words**, expert quotes with attribution, citing authoritative sources, info-dense short paragraphs/lists, first-party data, E-E-A-T, topical authority (pillar+cluster+reciprocal internal linking, because AI "query fan-out" reads the whole site), freshness (the **~90-day "citation cliff"**), and off-page/earned media (brands 6.5× more likely cited via third-party sources; digital PR lifts AI citations a median 239%).
- **Reality check:** GEO/AEO is a layer **on top of** solid SEO, not a replacement (Google ~14B queries/day vs ChatGPT ~37.5M, ~373:1). Do both.
- **Measurement:** AI-referred sessions as separate GA4 channels; citation share-of-voice (Otterly.ai, Peec AI, Profound, Brandlight, LLMrefs); GSC AI-Overview impression share; branded-search lift. Timeline to set expectations: rankings 2–3mo, traffic 4–6mo, revenue 6–12mo.

**This dovetails exactly with the MOA marketing plan's §3.2 GEO/AEO push** — "be the cited answer for *NABH Ayurvedic hospital / panchakarma Kerala*" — and validates the factory's own spec-driven, maker-checker method (Part B/C of the KB).
