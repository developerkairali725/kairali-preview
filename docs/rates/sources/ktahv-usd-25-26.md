## Page 6 — TERMS & CONDITIONS

**Taxes & Additional Charges**
- All government taxes are included in our published fees; however, rates may change periodically.
- Please note that additional service charges may apply for optional services such as laundry, transportation, wellness sessions, medications, and extra meals.

**Booking & Payment Policies**
- Rate Validity: Our rates are quoted on a per-day basis and are effective from April 1 2025, through September 30, 2026.
- Reservation Deposit: A 40% deposit is required to secure your booking. The remaining balance must be settled at least 30 days prior to admission to confirm your reservation
- Room Requests: Requests for specific rooms must be made at least six months in advance, subject to availability, with full payment required for confirmation.

**Check-in & Check-out**
- Check-in: 12:00 noon
- Check-out: 10:00 AM
- Early check-in and late check-out are available upon request and may incur additional charges, subject to availability.

**Cancellation Policy**
- 30+ Days Before Admission: Full refund
- 15-30 Days Before Admission: 50% refund.
- Less than 15 Days Before Admission or Date Change During Stay: No refund

**Child Policy**
- Under 5 Years: Complimentary.
- Age 5 to 12 years: 50% charge for food and Extra bedding at standard rate.
- Over 12 Years: Full charge
- Parents or guardians are responsible for supervising children at all times. Please note that certain areas of our facility are designed primarily for wellness treatments and may not be child-friendly.

**Additional Policies for a Comfortable & Safe Stay**
- Respectful and Peaceful Environment : We are committed to maintaining a calm and healing atmosphere. We kindly ask all guests to keep noise levels low and respect the privacy and well-being of others.
