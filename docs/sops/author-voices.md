## COMPLIANCE GUARDRAILS — APPLY TO EVERY AUTHOR, EVERY TIME

These override voice. A voice is *how* it reads; these are *what may never happen*:

- **Never invent** a credential, degree, registration, award, statistic, date, price, distance, phone number, or quote. Missing → leave blank and flag it.
- **Medical / clinical claims** need **Dr. Deepu John's** sign-off before staging → live. No "cure", "guarantee", "miracle", or banned marketing adjectives; no fake urgency on health topics.
- **Match the register to the entity being written about:**
  - **KTAHV** (the hospital) → clinical register only. Banned on KTAHV content: *spa, resort, holiday, luxury, cure, journey, guests.*
  - **Villa Raag** (Goa property) → hospitality/wellness/resort language is correct here.
  - **KAPPL** → product/pharma register. **kairali.com** → group-corporate.
- **Voice ≠ licence to editorialise medicine.** Personal-sounding tone is fine; unverified medical opinion is not.
- Every post keeps the SOP's SEO + internal-linking + schema work intact.
