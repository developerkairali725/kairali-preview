## Cheat Sheet

Prompt-Builder Cheat Sheet

## Edits & Notes

Edits & Notes — what changed vs. the original

| Column | Original | Changed to | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lighting | Edge Lighting (another term for Rim Lighting) | High-Key Lighting (bright, even, minimal shadows) | Duplicate concept — "Edge Lighting" is just a synonym for "Rim Lighting" (already row 4). Swapped in High-Key Lighting, a distinct, commonly used style the list was missing. Revert to "Edge Lighting" if you want the literal source. |
| Style | Gopro / Action Cam Style | GoPro / Action Cam Style | Brand-name capitalization. |
| Camera + Lens | Tilt Shot (camera moves up or down vertically) | Tilt Shot (camera pivots up or down) | Optional precision tweak — a tilt is a rotation from a fixed point; the original wording can read like a crane/pedestal move. Revert if you prefer the source. |
| Camera + Lens | Pan Shot (camera moves left or right horizontally) | Pan Shot (camera pivots left or right) | Same — a pan is a horizontal rotation, not lateral travel. Revert if you prefer the source. |
| Camera + Lens (note) | Slow Motion (120fps) and Time-lapse sit under Camera + Lens | Left as-is | These are speed/time effects rather than framing or lens choices, but kept in place to match your layout. Split into their own column later if you want stricter categories. |
