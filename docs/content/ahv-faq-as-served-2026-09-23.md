## Topic: Understanding Ayurveda  (id topic-understanding-ayurveda)

### q-08 — What is Ayurveda, and how can it benefit me?
Ayurveda is a 5,000-year-old holistic healing system that originated in India. It focuses on achieving a balance between body, mind, and spirit through personalized therapies, nutrition, herbal treatments, and lifestyle practices. By addressing the root causes of imbalance rather than just symptoms, Ayurveda offers sustainable solutions for overall well-being and prevention of disease.

### q-09 — How can I incorporate Ayurvedic principles into my daily life?
Begin with simple adjustments to your routine: Wake up before sunrise and sleep by 10 PM Practice mindful eating with fresh, seasonal foods Stay hydrated with warm water throughout the day Incorporate gentle movement like yoga or walking Practice oil pulling and self-massage (abhyanga) with appropriate oils Adjust your diet according to your dominant dosha (body constitution) Our wellness consultants will provide personalized recommendations during your stay.

### q-10 — What are doshas, and why are they important?
Doshas are the three bodily energies— Vata (air and space), Pitta (fire and water), and Kapha (earth and water)—that govern all physical and mental processes. Understanding your unique dosha composition helps determine the most effective treatments, diet, and lifestyle practices for your constitution. At Kairali, we conduct a thorough dosha assessment upon arrival to customize your wellness program.

### q-11 — Are Ayurvedic doctors real doctors?
Absolutely. Ayurvedic physicians in India complete a rigorous five-and-a-half-year BAMS (Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery) degree program that includes clinical training in both Ayurvedic and modern medical practices. Many pursue additional specializations (MD in Ayurveda), requiring 3 more years of study. Ayurvedic medicine is recognized by the WHO and regulated by dedicated government bodies like India’s Ministry of AYUSH. For an in-depth look at the qualifications and standards of Ayurvedic doctors, please read our blog: “ Are Ayurvedic Doctors Real Doctors? “
