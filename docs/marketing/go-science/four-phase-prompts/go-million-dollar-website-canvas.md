# My Million Dollar Website Canvas — transcription

A one-page homepage WIREFRAME CANVAS: a fixed section order down the middle (drawn
as wireframe boxes), a left rail of per-section tips, a right rail for notes. Header
fields: **Primary Moa** · **Secondary Moa** · **Name** · **Group**. Right rail:
"Notes and insights" (ruled lines) and "Scribble your ideas". GO M[arketing] logo
bottom-right.

## Section order (top to bottom), with the left-rail guidance

1. **Top Nav Bar** — logo, 3–5 main navigation links and CTA button.
2. **Header** — Heading · Crisp Sub-heading · Main CTA text. Tips: the heading
   should ideally be **>10 words and outcome driven**; the sub-heading may have
   **how + proof point**; the **photo frame on the right side should have a strong
   visual**. (Wireframe: heading bars left, large image frame right, MAIN CTA
   button below, logo + nav chips on top.)
3. **Short Intro** — a strong heading that makes your MoA curious; first one or two
   lines speak about the MoA's challenges; the next one or two lines about how you,
   your products or your services help resolve the challenges.
4. **Proof strip** — number of years in business · countries you export to ·
   number of SKUs · team size · clients served · service centers etc. (Wireframe:
   NUMBER boxes with TEXT lines beneath, 4 across.)
5. **Products / Product Categories / Services / Procedures** — strong heading;
   **3–6 products** with photo + product/service/category title; each card carries
   a "VIEW DETAILS →" link. (Wireframe: two rows of three image cards.)
6. **Download / Watch Video** — products or service video, OR brochure / catalog /
   eBook download; video thumbnail or brochure/book cover on the LEFT; strong
   heading, one subheading or 2–3 pointers with CTA button on the RIGHT.
7. **Why you? / Benefits** — 3–4 points about why your customer should buy only
   from you; benefits of buying your products or services. (Wireframe: icon +
   TITLE + supporting text, three across.)
8. **Trust Strip** — clients' logos (add names). (Wireframe: "TRUSTED BY:" + six
   logo slots.)
9. **Testimonials** — results and wins shared by customers; also add client photo,
   name and company. Tip — **lead with numbers**, like: "Production increased by
   40%" · "20% more work done in less time" · "21 lakh saved in 6 months".
   (Wireframe: two wide testimonial cards with photo circle + NAME + DESIGNATION.)
10. **Primary CTA** — Book a demo / Book a discovery call. (Wireframe: "TAKE
    ACTION" band with HEADING + CTA button.)
11. **Footer** — your company logo · address strip with phone number and email
    address · primary links (products / services) · secondary links (About, Blog,
    Insights, Jobs, Why us, Downloads etc.) · MORE/OTHER LINKS · SOCIAL MEDIA
    LINKS · PRIVACY POLICY · DISCLAIMER · TERMS OF USE.

## Reading (agent assessment, not part of the source)

The canvas is the homepage projection of the GO sales science: header = MCB-style
outcome hook aimed at the MoA; proof strip + trust strip = Phase-1 authority;
short intro = problem/solution bridge (Phase-2 shape); testimonials = Phase-3
proof; download/free-stuff + primary CTA = Phase-4 CTA/IRO. Section order embeds
Attention→Trust→Desire→Action. Compare docs/design/what-is-beauty-owner-paper
§10 sequence — the two orders are compatible but not identical.
