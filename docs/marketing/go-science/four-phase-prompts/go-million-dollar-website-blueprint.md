# My Million Dollar Website Blueprint — transcription

The fill-in COMPANION WORKSHEET to the Canvas: "**Fill in the blank spaces given to
get your website homepage wireframe ready**." Header fields: **Name** · **Group**.
Where the Canvas fixes the section ORDER and layout, the Blueprint collects the
CONTENT for each section as a questionnaire.

## Fields (numbered as printed)

1. **Top Bar** — write menu link names (blanks) · CTA Button Text / Contact no.
2. **Header** — Main heading · Sub-heading · Primary CTA · Secondary CTA.
3. **Problem / Challenges of Your Moa** — blanks 1–5, side by side with
   **Solution / Desired Outcome** — blanks 1–5.
4. **Your Story** — "How you solve your moa's challenges?" · "Why are you in this
   business" · "Key people who are leading" · "Brief overview" — side by side with
   **Proof / Results / Key Highlights** — Years in business · Office/factory size ·
   No. of SKUs · Team size · Dealers network · Sales figure · No. of countries you
   export · No. of clients served · Other (add yourself).
5. **Trusted by** — "Add name of client logos you can put" (two columns of blanks)
   — side by side with **Testimonials** — 1–3, each with "Name, designation and
   company".
6. **Products / Services** — list blanks — side by side with **Why Choose You?** —
   blanks 1–6.
7. **Free stuff (eBook, Catalog, Report)** — Heading · Key points 1–3 · CTA button
   text — side by side with **Main CTA / Desired Outcome** — Heading · Support
   text · CTA button text — and **FAQs** (blanks).
8. **Footer** — Contact no. · Email · Imp Links.

## Reading (agent assessment, not part of the source)

Blueprint fields map 1:1 onto Canvas sections: this is the INTAKE instrument, the
Canvas is the LAYOUT instrument. Together they are Akshar's Mode-A-style design
intake for a homepage: problem/solution pairs (MoA challenges → desired outcomes),
proof inventory, testimonial capture with attribution, one free-stuff lead magnet,
one main CTA. Direct candidates for improving our `launches/<domain>/design-intake/`
questionnaire and the strategy-gate payload — as inputs to improve on, not to
copy (owner caveat, D-20260720-11).
