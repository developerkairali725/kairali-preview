Menu July 2017


Healing Recipes is a three-day food adventure with India’s finest chefs, who will showcase the theatrics of good food through thoughtfully created Masterclasses.

Schedule 3 Nights / 4 Days

Day 1

Check in: Traditional Welcome at Kairali- The Ayurvedic Healing Village

Lunch: Kerala “Sadhya”, a traditional festive meal served on banana leaf.

3.30 pm: First meeting at the Culinary Cove with the directors of Kairali, Chefs, guests and invited guests followed by High Tea.

4.30 pm onwards: Meeting with Kairali doctors, followed by therapies as recommended.

6.00 pm: Group Meditation session

7.30 pm: Masterclass by Chef Vikas Seth (Topic: Localising Global Cuisine- Mexico)

Dinner.

Day 2

6.30 am: Group Yoga Session

7.30 am: Back to Roots Short Classes: Growing your own vegetable at the farm with Kairali’s resident horticulturist, G Anandan and Chef Abhijit Saha.

11 am: Panel discussion: Chef Manjit Singh Gill and Gita Ramesh (JMD – Kairali Ayurvedic Group) on the virtues of Vedic Eating

Noon: Masterclass by Chef Gill (Topic: The Culture of Taste- Heroing Moringa).

1.30 pm: Lunch cooked based on the Ayurvedic Cookbook.

2.30 pm: Kairali Ayurvedic Factory visit.

6.00 pm: Group Meditation

7.30 pm: Masterclass by Chef Arun Kumar TR (Topic: Food Resilience and the Art of Constructing a Dish).

Dinner.

Day 3

6.30 am: Group Yoga session

8.15 am: Masterclass by Chef Abhijit Saha (Topic: Superfood India).

10.15 am: Therapies sessions.

11.30 am: Village tour followed by a traditional local vegetarian meal.

6 pm: Group Meditation

7.30 pm: Panel discussion (Healing Foods: Imbibing the learning in our daily routine).

8.00 pm: Chefs-curated, sit-down dinner prepared by Chef Vikas Seth, Chef Abhijit Saha and Chef Arun Kumar TR.

Day 4

7.30 am: Ayurvedic therapies.

9 am: Back to Roots Session 2: Harvesting Spices at the Herb Garden with Chef Vikas Seth and resident horticulturist, G Ananadan

10 am: Breakfast and photo ops with chefs and others members of Kairali.

11 am: Departure of guests.

Other Complimentary services

Library & Recreational Room

Gym & Fitness centre

Swimming pool

Badminton & Tennis Court

Other Indoor & Outdoor Games

Palmistry, Astrology and Griha Pooja
