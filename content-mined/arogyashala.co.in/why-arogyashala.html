Why Arogyashala

  • Home
  • Why Arogyashala

The traditional Indian healing science Ayurveda, is today no longer as traditional and confined to India as it used to be earlier. Today, Ayurveda has spread all across the globe and people all over the world areusing the benefits of Ayurveda to relieve themselves from diseases and disorders without any side effects.

ArogyaShala is an endeavour of the Kairali Ayurvedic Products to make this amazing healing system and the Kairali Ayurvedic medicines and products available at your doorstep.

Concept

ArogyaShala is an initiative by Kairali Ayurvedic Products to make Ayurvedic cures and medicines available to all.

Anyone who is an ayurvedic practitioner can take ArogyaShala agency to increase income status and fulfil requirements of day to day medicines and ayurvedic products.

We will also help and update you with the information about the usage of all our Medicines and products

It will be very convenient for the patients to procure all kinds of medicines and ayurvedic products of the highest quality at one door step.

ArogyaShala is your opportunity to own, manage, and lead a personal business without having to take all the associated risks of a business. This is your opportunity to open your own business and have financially independent life.

Benefits of ArogyaShala Agency:

  • Kairali Ayurveic Group shall offer you all the aids and assistance needed in setting up an ArogyaShala Agency exclusively for Kairali’s Ayurvedic Product’s range which is already well established in the domestic and international market.
  • The risks associated are very less as compared to starting your own business from scratch as Kairali Ayurvedic products already has a brand name in the market.
  • We shall help and guide you with a proven and efficient method of business operation. Kairali has decades of business experience and we shall offer you management assistance and training, marketing assistance and leads, and also train you in Ayurvedic business ways.
  • We shall help to run local campaigns with very attractive fliers and pamphlets of ArogyaShala, free of cost. All the fliers and pamphlets will be developed and designed by our professional advertising team.

We will also do the designing and printing of the sign board in flex and supply the same to you.

With all the above mentioned aids your chances of running a successful business with minimum investment is guaranteed.

ArogyaShala is all about MINIMUM Investment for MAXIMUM Returns.

Eligibility:
  1. A room in the existing clinic or near by the clinic with as size of 10feet x 12feet.
  2. An initial investment of Rs. 50,000 (Fifty Thousand only) towards the cost of Medicines
  3. A Franchisee Agreement to be executed with Kairali Products Pvt Ltd.
  4. A Refundable Deposit of Rs.5000.00 (interest free) to Kairali Products Pvt Ltd.
  5. Colour scheme of ArogyaShala to be maintained.

AryogyaShala Agency

  • Any Ayurvedic doctor who has a clinic/treatment room can take a ArogyaShala Agency
  • Any Ayurvedic Doctor’s Clinic with an existing agency
  • Any entrepreneur can apply for a ArogyaShala agency and appoint an Ayurvedic doctor to escalate the sale of the products time consultation and prescription.
