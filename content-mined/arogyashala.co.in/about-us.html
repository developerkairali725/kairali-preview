ArogyaShala is all about the practice of the ancient Indian system of healing Thru Ayurveda. Ayurveda and Yoga are two interrelated branches of the same great tree of Vedic knowledge that encompasses all of human life and the entire universe. These are the two oldest and most ancient sciences in the whole world, the practice of Yoga and Ayurveda can provide a complete solution for stress-free living and healing without any side effects.

ArogyaShala is an attempt to make the procedures, products and medicines of these ancient sciences of healing available to the masses.

Ayurveda emphasizes more on prevention of diseases rather than its cure. The treatments provided in Ayurveda does not focus only to the ailment and the affected part, but to the person as a whole. Ayurveda focuses on treating the root cause of the disease and creates an environment for cleansing and purifying the body by elimination of all toxic imbalances of the body. Ayurveda addresses all aspects of healing including medicines, diet, herbs, herbal massage and lifestyle, enabling to regain natural resistance from diseases and attain good health.

Yoga helps achieve good health of the mind and body through the practice of asanas and meditation, along with breathing techniques.
