 ABOUT US

 Bespoke makeup artist Sneha Chowdhary has always had a passion for the arts and she realised her canvas is the face. Her passion has driven her to keep refining her skills and keeping up with trends, she constantly is educating herself. She has completed courses with Chandini Singh, and is graduating from the Scandinavian Makeup Academy.

 Specialising in Bridal & Fashion & Fantasy as well as Stage Makeup Creative Makeup Artistry, she understands the nuances of colour combinations and skin tone management. 

 Bridal Makeup: E​very brides vision of what she should look like is uniquely her own. It all starts with a one-on-one consultation, where every detail from your dress, jewellery, occasion to your location will be taken into consideration. She will also assess your skin and make helpful recommendations if needed. She offers an on-site bridal makeup artist and will happily come to you wherever you and your ladies will be getting ready. Her goal is for you to look and feel like the very best version of yourself on your big day.

 Sneha is constantly inspired by her clients and the creative collaborators she works alongside. With makeup as her tool she's all about helping women look their best and feel confident for special occasions and every day.
