 Bridal makeup

 Blush Lounge is a wellness studio for all would be brides who are given a chance to look the best on their ‘Big-Special-Day’. Different makeovers with different trending styles are provided by our team with proper consultation and analysis of the clientele. We start the process with a one on one bridal consultation where every aspect of the look is discussed and finalised. From analysing the skin to recommending products and treatments, the trial is an in-depth analysis of the clients skin. Following the trial, we ensure the bride feels and looks the best version of herself for her bridal functions. We understand the significance of this very day in her life and endeavour to contribute our 100%. From the makeup to her hairstyle, embellished with flowers and accessories we have it all sorted for her. Be it any bridal function, we have our team of experts who would deliver the best make-up and hairstyles for the bride. With different treatments available, each client is given utmost importance giving special attention to their skin. An in-house nail technician in provided on request. Blush Lounge makes it a point to make the day completely special for memorable for her.
